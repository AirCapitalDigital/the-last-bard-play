# Patch notes

Newest first. Each release also has its own file in `docs/patch_notes/`.

- [Alpha 0.4.6](patch_notes/v0.4.6-alpha.md): foes that catch fire, hats that cover hair, card stacks in the chest opening, new menu and Crescendo sounds, arrows for every note trail, a Lute Goblin that is easier to catch, readable biome foes, fewer meadow villages, and a long list of controller and menu fixes.
- [Alpha 0.4.5](patch_notes/v0.4.5-alpha.md): the desert biome (the Parched Reach), igloos, new biome foes (the Lute Goblin thief, wolves, scarecrows, choristers, cantors, burrowers), the Wailer choir, a choice when every card is taken (Loot Mode), power-up callouts, scarcer loot, tougher Staccatos and Behemoths, gentler talking wards, and a Crescendo that sounds like one.
- [Alpha 0.4.4](patch_notes/v0.4.4-alpha.md): the Leaderboard opens (eight boards, headshots, weapon, boost and gear icons, device icons, podium stars), a Game stats tab, a title Discord button and Links, version names and a calmer floor glow.
- [Alpha 0.4.3](patch_notes/v0.4.3-alpha.md): a Halloween camp, vanity items (Pumpkin Head), bard names and a coming-soon Leaderboard, a fuller pause-menu stat list, a tougher full Fountain, thinner late Echoes and a regen ceiling.
- [Alpha 0.4.2](patch_notes/v0.4.2-alpha.md): a fully grown Fountain now brings a much bigger horde.
- [Alpha 0.4.1](patch_notes/v0.4.1-alpha.md): boss immunities and resistances, Revive first on the death screen, and fixes for stuck keys, switcher arrows and the death list.
- [Alpha 0.4.0](patch_notes/v0.4.0-alpha.md): the Mirror (skin, hair, eyes, belt and hairstyle, with looks won by achievements), a Fountain that grows in front of you, a readable Crescendo button and low-health warning, and many fixes.
- [Alpha 0.3.0](patch_notes/v0.3.0-alpha.md): Steam Deck support, tabbed settings, a six-stage Fountain, the Drunken Monkey, evolution flourishes, an icon for every piece of gear and a tougher late game.
- [Alpha 0.2.2](patch_notes/v0.2.2-alpha.md): every weapon can evolve, a one-piece armory mannequin, companion cards and boosts, much more of Maestra's lore, and a smooth browser build.
- [Alpha 0.2.1](patch_notes/v0.2.1-alpha.md): playing-card level-ups, the Sanctuary rebuilt as a castle, new HUD and death screen, menus that follow the layout, and many fixes.
- [Alpha 0.2.0](patch_notes/v0.2.0-alpha.md): evolution scenes, True bosses, the late game, six upgrade slots, loading screen, hover cards and a fresh look for the menus.
- [Alpha 0.1.1](patch_notes/v0.1.1-alpha.md): keyboard polish and menu fixes.
- [Alpha 0.1.0](patch_notes/v0.1.0-alpha.md): the first numbered build (Sanctuary castle, Journal, title screen, new music, Magic Harp, builds and synergies).
