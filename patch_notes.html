The Last Bard: Alpha 0.4.6

Fire catches, hats cover heads, the camp learns to behave with a controller, and the new foes get their teeth.

New

  • Fire is visible. Some of the foes hit by the Ember Bolt (and anything fused from it), the mage companion or the Infernal Crescendo now catch light for a moment, with little flames flickering over their shoulders.
  • The chest opening shows what you won. Each pick rises out of the chest as a small flared stack of three treasure cards instead of a glowing orb: a silver chest shows two stacks, a gold one three.
  • New sounds. A coin ping when you buy something with Echoes (and a falling ping when you take a level back), a soft clunk and chime when you equip gear, a pet, a College, a Crescendo score or a look, and a gentle chime when the Crescendo has recharged.
  • Arrows for every trail of notes. The chevron at the bard's feet that leads you to the cape's Husk now also points along each trail of notes, in the trail's own colour: Kage's red, the Resonant Stone's violet, the ruined College's gold, the ranger's green, the mage's blue.
  • NEW tags in the Mirror. The looks and vanity you have just unlocked wear a gold NEW tag the next time you open it, so the "!" over the Mirror tells you what it was for.
  • The Artifacts tab is an inventory, like the Collection: a tile for each artifact and a panel with its story. The little pedestals round the Fountain are gone.

Changes

  • Headgear covers the head. Hoods, coifs and helms now hide the hair under them, and caps, berets and straw hats hide the top of it, on the bard in the run and on the avatar in the menus.
  • The Lute Goblin is easier to catch. A pulsing ring (gold while it hunts, red once it runs and can be hurt) and sparkles mark it, and it leaves a trail of fading coins and orbs (not real loot) behind it. It stays near the screen for at least 16 seconds before it may run, and runs a little slower.
  • Foes you could not read now show their hand. The Scarecrow Choir never moves: it stands on its stake and sings at you from almost off the screen. The Bone Cantor stands still and chants, with a purple ring and "ARISE!", before its skeletons climb out, and is tougher. The Sand Burrower starts its attack from farther away, tunnels faster and cannot be hurt while it tunnels, marks a bigger ring where you are heading, shouts "ERUPTS!", then stays exposed a moment longer. The Meadow Goblin throws flaming daggers.
  • Bosses, new foes and choirs keep their distance from each other. A boss's arrival, a new foe's first showing and a Wailer choir are now kept 30 to 45 seconds apart, so the pop-ups never pile on.
  • Fewer villages and tents in the meadow, and the hedgehog's chances are doubled to make up for it, so he is still easy to find.
  • The screen-clearing power-up is now called the Sun Burst.
  • The Horde-Breaker achievement (and the Flame hair it unlocks) now asks for 20,000 foes in a single run.
  • The camp's Crescendo has a short 3-second cooldown again, so it cannot flood the courtyard.
  • The Leaderboard opens on All versions, so a new version never looks like a reset.

Controller and menus

  • Up and Down in a menu now stay in their own column or grid until it runs out, so a button beside a list is never the "next row": no more skips to the Headgear button in the Mirror, or sideways on the death screen.
  • After equipping something in the Collection, the selection stays on that tile instead of jumping to the top.
  • Settings: Down from the last row now reaches Resume, Exit to title and Quit game.
  • The pause menu opens with Resume selected.
  • In the camp, A no longer fires the Crescendo (R2 does), and the SET OUT, MENU and mute buttons hide while you use a controller.
  • The key hints no longer flicker between keyboard and controller names (E and R2).
  • The bard turns smoothly through its facings instead of popping.

Fixes

  • Maestra no longer vanishes a couple of seconds before her last line and her ward end: she leaves in a flash of light at the same moment as her ward.
  • The tip about the final boss now names the Closer.

The Last Bard: Alpha 0.4.5

The Parched Reach opens, a choir of wailers sings together, and loot gets harder to come by.

New

  • New foes that live in one biome each. The meadow has the Meadow Goblin (charges in a zigzag and throws daggers), the Scarecrow Choir (asleep until you come near, then it sings after you) and, rarely, the Lute Goblin: a thief that races for your XP orbs, Echoes and loot chests, grabs a batch or three at a time (a pop-up says what), cannot be hurt while it is busy, then bolts for the edge of the screen. Catch it before it is gone and half of what it took spills back. The forest has Wolf Husks that hunt in packs of three (a howl, then a rush), the snow has the Frost Chorister (a cone of cold, then frost notes that slow you), the crypt has the Bone Cantor (raises skeletons) and the desert has the Sand Burrower (a ring on the ground, then it erupts under you).
  • A desert biome: the Parched Reach. Sand, pale dunes, cracked clay and the odd small oasis, with cacti, barrel cacti, dry brush, tumbleweeds, sandstone buttes, obelisks, broken columns and bleached ribs. Palms stand only in the oases and reeds grow only at the water. No villages here: only abandoned camps with cold fire rings, sandstone tower ruins and the occasional palm grove. Its own breakable, the Desert Amphora, sits beside the crates and pots.
  • Igloos in the snow, scattered about and as the main buildings of the snow settlements. The forest now has cabins and trapper camps instead of full villages.
  • The Wailer choir. From about 6:30 a ring of wailers lands round you and sings together: sometimes all in one volley, sometimes as a turning wave so the notes spiral in. It grows by a singer every 40 seconds.
  • A choice when every card is taken. The first time everything is at its top level, a screen asks what your level-ups should give from now on: Endless Boosts (for the leaderboards: the three endless cards, forever) or Loot Mode (for the treasure hunter: level-ups stop pausing the game, pay Echoes, may drop a small chest, and your chests hold 30% more gear). Change it any time in Settings > Gameplay.
  • Power-up callouts. A bouncy line over the bard says what a power-up just did ("HUSH: the horde freezes for 5 s"). On by default, with a switch in Settings > Gameplay.
  • The Crescendo button shows in the camp, with no cooldown there, and its colour follows the rarity of the score you wear (a Perfected one is orange). The Crescendo itself now sounds like one: a chord swelling to a boom.
  • Restore defaults at the foot of every Settings page.

Changes

  • Loot is scarcer. Chests hold gear far less often (bronze 12%, silver 22%, gold 38%, boss 55%), and rarity now follows the run: rare from about 3:00, epic from 8:00, legendary from 15:00, mythic from 30:00. Commons and uncommon fill first; the top rarities take many runs. Anything can still drop at any time, just rarely.
  • Quiet parts of a song thin the horde by at most 20% from about 7:00 on (early on they stay a real breather).
  • Staccato foes now tense at their own distances and rest for different times, so a group never lunges together and the red lane ends near you; golden ones dash farther. Bass Behemoths are tougher. Gold foes climb to five stars.
  • Phantom Crescendo: about a quarter of the foes near the phantom walk toward it instead of you.
  • The talking wards (Maestra, Kage, Rowan, Morwen): silent now, softer to look at, fewer projectiles that each hit harder, and whoever was talking leaves in a flash of light at the same moment as their ward.
  • Tougher early game and bosses: more wailers and discords as a run goes on, golems arrive sooner, and the Choir fires three aimed waves and has more health.
  • The Fountain on the Upgrades page is now the Fountain icon and "Lv. 1/6" (the bird bath is level 1). The floor glow is softer.
  • Boost tags (DMG, CRT, SPD...) are three letters at most and sit at the icon's corner. The Wider Chord has a new picture, and the Final Flourish note is bigger and easier to see.
  • The Mirror says how a look was unlocked. Pet pop-ups no longer mention the tent.
  • The Leaderboard shows what ended a run (a skull) and how grown the Fountain was.

Fixes

  • Gold foes' stars sat far above their heads and foes were drawn slowly after the pumpkin Husk was added: fixed.
  • On touch screens, tapping an icon on the death screen, the pause menu or the Leaderboard opens its card and tapping it again closes it; the Return to camp button no longer flashes, and cards no longer stay on screen after a menu closes.
  • The Leaderboard no longer reports a Crescendo before you have found it.
  • Boss arrival messages no longer give away which weapon families they resist before you can use them.

The Last Bard: Alpha 0.4.4

The Leaderboard is open: name your bard, climb the boards, and see how everyone plays.

New

  • The Leaderboard is live. Give your bard a name and every run you finish counts. There are eight boards: Records (longest run, most kills, highest level, most bosses) and Totals (most deaths, most runs, most playtime, total kills). Scroll as deep as the board goes, jump to your own spot with Find me, and see your rank on every board on the You tab. The top three get a big gold, silver and bronze star.
  • Every entry shows how it was played. A little headshot of the bard (hair, skin, eyes, and the Pumpkin Head or party hat if worn, never gear), an icon for phone, PC or Steam Deck, the weapons carried, the companion, the pet and the Crescendo. One icon lists every boost card used and one lists every piece of gear worn. Hover (or tap) any icon for a card with the details.
  • Game stats. A new tab with the numbers behind the game, added up over everyone's runs: how many bards and hours, which weapons, boosts and gear are carried most and how long those runs last, how much damage each weapon deals, what ends runs and when. We are using this to balance the game, and you can see it too.
  • Find it two ways. A LEADERBOARD button on the title screen, and a bounty board in the camp, by the west wall (a wooden board with pinned notices, a trophy on a pedestal and bunting). Both open the same page and both ask for a name the first time, with a clear choice: save a name and continue, or opt out and share nothing.
  • A Discord server button on the title screen, and a Links section in Settings > General (Discord, patch notes, the itch.io page).
  • Version names. The title screen and the Sanctuary now show the version's name, like Alpha 0.4.4 "Hall of Bards".

Changes

  • A full Fountain's floor glow is toned down: it reaches about 8 tiles from the bard (down from about 11), and a first-stage Fountain about 3.
  • The Wider Chord boost has a new picture: a big gold note with rings spreading out from it, no longer something that looks like a wifi signal.
  • The camp's Leaderboard slab and the title screen's button show a gold ! until you have saved a name or opted out.
  • Settings > Gameplay has a switch to share your run results (on by default). Runs played with developer controls, god mode, auto-play or changed difficulty settings are never ranked.

Fixes

  • Your runs are reported when they really end (leaving the death screen, ending the run or quitting). Short early autosaves used to swallow them.
  • The name box in the Leaderboard pop-up keeps typing focus, and its border is no longer cut off.
  • Pressing E (or Enter) on a Leaderboard tab no longer throws focus down to the bottom button: you stay on the tab and can go left or right.
  • The Leaderboard page keeps one fixed size, so nothing jumps when you press a button.

The Last Bard: Alpha 0.4.3

Spooky season, a bard with a name, a tougher full Fountain and a lot of smoothing.

New

  • Halloween. From mid October to early November the camp turns into a night-time haunt: jack-o'-lanterns, pumpkin stacks, tombstones, candles, a bubbling cauldron and bats overhead. Pumpkins and candles turn up out in the world too, and now and then a Husk shambles in wearing a pumpkin (it bites like any other). Settings > Gameplay has a switch for seasonal decorations.
  • Vanity items. Play during Halloween and you keep the Pumpkin Head (glowing eyes) for good, and it comes back every year. The Drunken Monkey's party hat is a vanity item now too. Wear either from the new VANITY row at the Mirror: it changes how your head piece looks, never what it does. Hover the violet badge on a piece of gear to see what is drawn over it and where it came from.
  • Name your bard. Pick a bard name (and change it any time at the Mirror). There is a Leaderboard slab in the camp, by the west wall near the Journal: it explains it all the first time. The boards themselves are coming soon. Runs you play now are being kept, and they will count once it opens. Runs played with developer controls, god mode, auto-play or changed difficulty settings are never ranked. Settings > Gameplay has a switch for sharing your run results.
  • The pause menu's Run tab shows much more. THE RUN is three short rows, and THE BARD now lists every bonus: max health, regen, armor, damage, attack speed, reach, crit chance and damage, move speed, pickup range, XP, luck, revives, and the Crescendo and cymbal bonuses when you have them.

Changes

  • A full Fountain now makes foes tougher too, not just more numerous: up to twice the health at a fully grown Fountain, on top of the bigger horde. A maxed bard should have to work for it.
  • Echoes thin out late in a run. Small Echo drops (crates, pots, ordinary kills) start to taper after 8 minutes, down to 30% of their usual amount from about 23 minutes. The 10, 20 and 30 minute bonuses and boss rewards are unchanged.
  • Health regeneration has a ceiling. Everything that adds regen (gear, pets, Mending) still stacks, but the bard never heals faster than 2 health a second. Fully decked-out gear was healing far too fast. Mending's text now says so.
  • A locked-in chest card means plain chests. While a card is locked in, chests just give their picks: no gear, Crescendos or sheet music drop from them. Unlock the card in Settings to loot normally again.
  • Reprieve is switched off for now while live audio is ironed out. The music keeps playing and the horde keeps coming.
  • The RESIST tag now shows properly next to IMMUNE.
  • A lot of work on speed: far fewer draw calls in busy late-game fights, and decoration that steps down for a moment when the frame rate drops.
  • Phones: Listen mode no longer feeds the game its own sound back, and is more robust on Android 14.

Fixes

  • Tapping Equip on a gear card in the loadout on a touch screen closed the card instead of equipping. It equips now.
  • Pet icons are no longer cut off in their boxes (the monkey's cymbals, and many pets on a phone).

The Last Bard: Alpha 0.4.2

Max Fountain players asked for a real fight. Here it is.

Short one, and a spicy one. A lot of you have the Fountain fully grown and were bored by the late game, so the Fountain now pays you back with a much bigger horde.

Changes

  • A grown Fountain brings a bigger horde. The more of the Fountain of Echoes you own, the more foes turn up: about 60% more at half the Fountain, and 3.5 times as many with all of it. It grows gently at first and ramps up near the top, and it applies to the beat waves, the steady trickle and the big surges alike.
  • If this feels like too much (or not enough), tell me. It is a single number I can turn.

The Last Bard: Alpha 0.4.1

Bosses with weaknesses, and a pile of fixes from your notes.

Short and sweet this time. The big new thing is that some of the nastiest foes now shrug off certain weapons, so what you carry matters.

New

  • Immunities and resistances. Bosses and the Bone Amalgam are no longer equally squishy to everything. A small tag rises off them in the weapon family's colour (with its little symbol) when your hit is blocked.

- The Drum Lord is immune to percussion and resists blades (half damage).

- The Choir is immune to voice and resists spells (35% less).

- The Phantom Orchestra is immune to strings and resists bows (25% less).

- The Bone Amalgam is immune to blades.

- Companions, the Crescendo and damage over time always land, and wind weapons are never blocked. If everything you carry is immune to a boss, it still takes a quarter damage, so no build gets stuck. The arrival message and the Compendium tell you what each one is immune to.

Changes

  • Revive comes first. On the death screen the highlighted button is Revive whenever you have one, so a click a moment too early cannot send you back to camp.
  • Pets show up on the death screen. A pet you win mid-run (the Drunken Monkey, for example) is now listed under "New in your collection".
  • Carried boosts keep coming. A boost you already carry stays on offer until it is maxed, even if the weapon or companion that unlocked it is gone.
  • The achievement for trying every boost now says "boost card".

Fixes

  • A click on the left arrow of a "< value >" setting now goes back (it always went forward, so songs and other lists seemed shuffled).
  • Fixed a stuck key: if a menu closed while you were still holding S (or any of W, A, D, or the stick), the bard could keep walking on its own after returning to camp.

The Last Bard: Alpha 0.4.0

Make your bard yours: a Mirror to change skin, hair, eyes and more, a Fountain that grows right in front of you, a Crescendo button you can read at a glance, and a long list of fixes from your notes.

Quick one after 0.3, and a fun one. A lot of this came straight from your feedback again. Thank you, keep it coming.

New

  • The Mirror. Your bard finally gets to look like your bard. Hit CUSTOMIZE under the bard on the title screen, or walk up to the standing mirror in the corner of camp by the tent and the wall tower. Pick skin, hair, eyes, belt colour and hairstyle, with a live preview that follows you everywhere the bard is drawn: runs, camp, loadout, pause menu and title.
  • Wild looks are won, not bought. Rainbow hair, flame hair, starlight hair, ghost skin, glowing eyes, spiky "Wild" hair and more are unlocked by achievements you already know (find every pet, reach level 50, survive 30 minutes...). Hover a locked swatch to see how to win it.
  • The Fountain of Echoes grows right in front of you. Spend your Echoes, close the menu, and the fountain climbs through every stage you just bought, one at a time, each with a ring of light, sparks and a chime. Each stage also adds a bird on the rim now, up to six.
  • Something is lighting the way. If you wander near a lost Magic Harp, you might notice a few small violet lanterns scattered about. No arrow, no trail. Just a reason to go and look.
  • Show the bard's helmet (Settings > Gameplay): turn it off and no headwear is drawn anywhere. Your helmet stays equipped and keeps its stats.

Changes

  • The Crescendo button is readable from the corner of your eye. The wait now sits big over the note, disappears the moment it is ready, and the button glows harder with a ring that swells out. Its colour follows the Crescendo you have equipped, on desktop and phone, and the wave's inner ring takes the colour too.
  • Health and XP bars react. A heart or health bonus sweeps a bright band along the HP bar, and an XP magnet (power-up or Crescendo) does the same on the XP bar. When your health gets low the screen edges throb red and the bar throbs harder the lower you go (steady with Reduce flashing).
  • Music follows the moment. It rises slowly instead of jumping at the start of a run. When the bard falls, the song slows and sinks and the camera leans in, and it stays that way until you are back in camp, where it winds back up.
  • A chest holds one find. Never two gear pieces or a piece and a Crescendo any more, plus at most one sheet of music. It keeps the chest screen tidy.
  • Maestra leaves before the College callout, and her guard stands down. The "College selected" line now waits until the field is clear.
  • The armory's relic stand is closer to the mannequin and shows the relic's own picture circling the plinth. The relic circling the bard on the menu avatars is its picture too.
  • The upgrades page fits on one screen with no cut-off rows, and shows how many Echoes you have spent next to REFUND ALL. The Echoes counters have the Echoes icon.
  • Achievement badges wait for the camp's own line under the timer before popping up.
  • Cleaner camp and world: the crypt floor lost its odd purple rune marks, haybales and barrels keep a tile away from houses, and the beehives, crates and pots that were left standing after a run no longer follow you into the Sanctuary.

Fixes

  • Maestra's list of questions no longer closes the whole chat when you press Enter, Space, E or the pad's A. The first option is highlighted so Up, Down and Enter work.
  • CUSTOMIZE on the title screen can be reached with the keyboard and the pad.
  • The Muted Crescendo's text is short and simple everywhere it is shown.

The Last Bard: Alpha 0.3.0

A Steam Deck that actually fits and names its own buttons, settings in proper tabs, a grander Fountain of Echoes, a drunken new friend, evolutions that finally look the part, a picture for every piece of gear, and a late game that bites back.

Big one this time. A lot of this came straight from your feedback and from me sitting with a Deck in my hands for a few days. Thank you to everyone who sent notes, screenshots and logs. Keep them coming, they really do end up in the game.

New

  • A drunken monkey pet. The Drunken Monkey carries a cymbal in each hand and gives +1 cymbal to Cymbal Crash and its evolutions. Survive 30 minutes in one run with Cymbal Crash at max level (or evolved) to win him over. He is rumoured to throw a party in camp.
  • The Fountain of Echoes grows through six stages (one more bird perches on its rim at each stage), from a bird bath with a sparrow on the rim to a grand fountain with a pillar of golden light, spiralling jets and drifting orbs. The golden glow on the floor round the bard now belongs to the Fountain: none on a fresh bard, noticeable half-way, the full floor at the end.
  • A picture for every piece of gear. All 97 pieces have their own drawn icon: hoods, vests, boots, cloaks, relics and charms (some cloaks used to be boots). Headwear is shown as worn: hoods wrap the bard's head, caps and hats sit on it, crowns stand above it, a helm covers the face and a laurel rings it, in the camp, in a run and on the loadout bard.
  • Evolutions look the part. Each twin evolution now hits at full strength, fires 12% faster, reaches a little further and, when both halves strike together, rings out in a flourish of its own colour and sparkle (every fifth cast for the single-weapon evolutions). The other evolutions hit 10% harder.

Steam Deck and controllers

  • Fits the Deck's screen: fullscreen with no black bars on a 16:10 display.
  • Your pad's own button names: hints name the buttons of an Xbox, PlayStation, Switch or Steam Deck pad (A / Cross / B / A, L1, R2 and so on). Pick one yourself in Settings > Controls.
  • A quicker walk in the camp (a flat speed, 30% above the starting one, whatever gear you wear; pets and companions keep up).
  • A party hat for the Drunken Monkey, and the bard wears one too while the monkey is along. It only changes how the bard looks: whatever you have equipped stays worn with its stats, and a small violet sparkle badge on that piece in the loadout, the pause menu and the Collection says its look is being replaced.
  • The Ballad shows its notes on the loadout and pause-menu bard, and the bard holds nothing (and nothing glows in the hands) while it is the starting weapon.
  • The light above the Fountain of Echoes is always there, a gentle column that grows taller with the fountain, and a tall, bright beam with a bright core and a ring round its foot when you can afford an upgrade.
  • Show the frame rate (Settings > Graphics) puts a small readout in the corner.
  • Down and Up walk every row of every menu, sliders and switches included: a menu never jumps over a row any more (a pad's d-pad used to skip some).
  • Linked tiles stay in view: in the Unlocks page, a weapon's fusions that are below the fold hop up to its edge, held by their strings, and settle back as you scroll.

Settings

  • Five tabs: Graphics, Gameplay, Controls, Audio and General. LB / RB flip through them.
  • New options: fullscreen, V-Sync, a frame-rate limit, a Controls page that lists the pad's buttons, and a switch for showing on Discord that you are playing.

Changes

  • Late game: the crowd is back. A full 30 minute run with max gear on the Deck showed way too few foes at the end, so waves grow faster late, enemies get tougher a little sooner, and the echo bosses have twice the health. If you were bored at minute 28, that should be fixed. Tell me if it is now too much.
  • Magnet Crescendo deals no damage: it only nudges the crowd back, and the pull on orbs and Echoes is the point.
  • Starting weapons are the original bard set again: lute, pan flute, war drum, tambourine, rally horn, harp of light and ballad. The bard in the loadout and the pause menu holds the one you chose (the title screen bard keeps the lute).
  • Takashi's Ocarina: the Ocarina is renamed in honour of a playtester.
  • The Crescendo button has a bigger note, glowing rings and a slow pulse while it is ready (phone and desktop).
  • Companion boosts wear a small sword, bow or flame in the corner of their card and tile.
  • The songs you always have with the Magic Harp are now Lonely Espresso and Stompwood Run (with the Overture), listed first. Hearthside Reel and Greywall Hymn are found as torn sheet music like the others.
  • Crescendo cards are aqua (gold was too close to the String family), show the Crescendo's own mark (a note in swelling rings) with a small symbol for what each upgrade does, and the Wider Chord icon no longer looks like a Wi-Fi sign.
  • Cape gauge (Settings > Gameplay) shows a small picture of the cape filling for each choice.
  • The golden light above the Fountain of Echoes (when you can afford an upgrade) now reaches all the way down to the fountain's foot, fading out near it, so it never stops short on the small fountains.
  • The pet you wear follows you round the camp. Mote the Orb has a new line.
  • The companion offer card no longer talks about opening a College or weapons (those are already yours from meeting them); it only says who will fight beside you in this run. Foes that fall during a companion's meeting drop no XP, Echoes or loot.
  • Companions and Maestra guard you properly while they talk. It was too weak to even notice before. Now: a glowing ward ring round them, a pulse that rolls out every second and wipes the ordinary foes it crosses, and a barrage of their own weapon (Kage's whirling axes, Rowan's fans of arrows, Morwen's bursting orbs, Maestra's homing notes).
  • The Closer has a lot more health, and the message at 30 minutes now reads "IT GETS QUIET... TOO QUIET...". Sounds about right.
  • The Closer now has a health bar, so you can see how the fight is going.
  • Quick Fingers says what it does: weapons attack 12% faster per level.
  • Gear popups use a red minus for a stat the swap would drop, instead of a red X.
  • The death screen's Return to camp button glows like the other go buttons.
  • Small text is a little larger (nothing below 12 px except the XP bar's label).

Fixes

  • On the Deck, the d-pad and stick could stop moving between cards on the second set of treasure cards. Fixed (tell me if you still catch it).
  • The title screen no longer shows a strip of the game under it on 16:10 screens.
  • Menus no longer double-step or skip rows with a controller, and dropdowns are plain buttons you can cycle with left and right.

The Last Bard: Alpha 0.2.2

A browser build that stays smooth, weapons that can all evolve, a one-piece armory mannequin, companions with their own cards, and a lot more to ask Maestra.

New

  • Every weapon can evolve. Eleven new twin fusions join the six old ones, so none of the fifteen base weapons is left out. A twin fusion is two real weapons fighting as one, each firing a little faster and a little weaker: Hurricane Fanfare (Rally Horn + Chain Spark), Siren's Refrain (Ballad + Sheet Music), Prism Harp (Harp of Light + Ember Bolt), Duelist's Score, Brass Bolts, Pyre Arrows, Arc Repeater, Brass Thunder (Cymbal Crash + War Drum), Duelist's Duet, Grand Lyre (the lute and the harp) and Storm Choir. Each has a two-part icon, and the Unlocks menu previews it.
  • Kage has boosts of his own: Whetted Edge (his cuts hit 20% harder and reach wider) and Second Cut (a chance for an extra cut on each stroke). They appear once you have found him, like Rowan's and Morwen's.
  • Companion offer cards now tell you what each companion does. The go-alone card is Lone Wolf: +20 max health (with a little heart), healed at once, and it goes the moment you take a companion. All your companions wait for you back at camp.
  • Maestra Orsolya has a great deal more to say. Ask her about the Silence, the Closer, the husks, the castle, Oakhaven, the Resonant Stone, the little animals and the old treasures. Kage, Rowan and Morwen have more to tell too.

Changes

  • The armory is one mannequin that wears your cape, helm, breastplate and greaves, each in the colour of the best piece you own for that slot. A piece you have not found leaves that part bare. The relic and charm stands stay beside it.
  • Synergy strings in the Unlocks menu always flow from the boost to the weapon it boosts, whichever of the two is highlighted.
  • Power-ups no longer interfere: picking up a new one never shortens one that is still running.
  • Reprieves stay busy for a bard with the whole Fountain. The gap between songs now counts how much of the Fountain you own, not just what you spent, so a fully upgraded bard keeps a real crowd to fight.

Camp, strings and phones (from Oct 25)

Camp

  • A card book stands beside the Collection rack: an album whose card slots fill as you find weapon and boost cards, with loose cards leaning on it from half full and a golden glow when it is complete. Same station and menu as the rack.
  • The Resonant Stone moved to the camp's north-west, away from the armory.

Cards, strings and HUD
