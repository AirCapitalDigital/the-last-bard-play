# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.1.0
*The first numbered build. Everything below landed in the last 24 hours.*

## New: The Sanctuary
- The camp is now a walled castle. Walk out through the **gate** to start a run, no button needed.
- Your first run skips the camp; it opens after your first death.
- **The Journal** (one big book): Monsters, People, Bards College, Achievements and Stats in one place.
- **The Collection** cabinet: a new Unlocks page for every weapon, fusion and boost (hidden as ??? until found).
- The **Bards College** castle rebuilds, tower by tower, as you reopen Colleges. Walk to a tower and press E to equip it.
- **The Altar of Echoes** lights up when you can afford an upgrade.
- Your tent and the armory fill with chests, stands and pedestals as you collect gear.

## New: Title screen and music
- A full title screen with its own theme, an animated castle scene and a menu.
- **3 new soundtrack tracks** (Ember Road, Lonely Espresso, Midnight Gallop) alongside the original.
- **The Magic Harp**: a lost artifact hidden in the wild. Find it, then pick your song in camp.
- *Your Music (experimental)*: play one of your own songs instead.

## New: Loadout and builds
- Equipment screen with a paper doll and animated bard preview, icon grids and hover details.
- Swap gear mid-run from the pause menu, but only with pieces you found that run.
- Pause menu shows your **current build**: weapons, upgrades and the synergy lines between them.
- Weapons now hang around the bard and animate when they attack. The lute glows when the Crescendo is ready.
- Hover the Companion, Pet and Crescendo slots to see their stats for the run.

## Mobile and touch
- New touch layout: your gear and the Crescendo button sit at the **top** of the screen, so **either thumb** can steer with the on-screen stick.
- Big tap targets everywhere: PLAY, Reroll, Revive and Return to camp.
- Collection: **tap once to read, tap again to wear**. The Loadout works the same way.
- Every menu and the death screen are usable by touch as well as keyboard and mouse.

## Gameplay
- Siege rings of enemies appear at higher minutes.
- Upgrades are now per music type (strings, wind, percussion, voice, blades, bows, spells), each with damage and crit versions.
- Fusions stay secret until you make one yourself.
- Blades unlock with Kage. Bows and spells are reserved for later companions.
- The first level-up always offers at least two weapons.
- Harp of Lines now bursts on the ground. The Ballad aura flows outward in waves.
- Chests no longer drop duplicate gear.
- New **Lone Wolf** trait: walk alone (with a companion available) for +20 max health.
- Every biome has its own breakable: snowman, beehive, stump and burial urn.
- New pet: **Ajax the Shiba Inu**. Pet unlocks no longer pop up the instant a run starts.
- The Crescendo's shield is now violet and can overlap the power-up shield.

## Balance
- Kage hits less often and for less.
- Staccato shows a red lane before it dashes, and dashes farther.
- The Drum Lord now lunges (with a red warning lane) if you kite it.
- Unlock gates count deaths, not runs, so quitting early can't skip them.
- Companion camps only appear after their College is already open, 3 to 5 minutes into a run.
- Quest finds (Stone, College, camp, harp) are placed deeper inside biomes.
- Snare is a touch quieter on every song.

## Quality of life
- Every menu works with arrow keys, Enter/Space and Tab.
- Unlocks, Colleges, pets and foes pop up as achievement-style badges. Achievements are shown after a run.
- The death screen shows icons for damage, finds and achievements, with bigger buttons for touch.
- A red kick ring was recoloured so it no longer looks like damage.
- Touch players get the same pop-ups, hover cards (tap to read) and menus as everyone else.
- Menus share one look: matching headers, padding and fixed-size frames.

## Fixes
- Fixed tall props popping in at screen edges and a Collection hover jump.
- Fixed menu layouts shifting between tabs.
- Fixed Harp visuals showing before the harp was found.
- Fixed the Maestra's hall roof drawing as a floating slab.
