# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.2.0
*The big one: weapons that evolve, late-game bosses and waves, a lot of polish, and every menu given a fresh look.*

## New
- **Evolution scene**: fuse two weapons from a chest and they spin round each other, a beat of silence, then *pop*: the new weapon appears. Evolved weapons are gilded around the bard and each shows what it grew from.
- **Synergies on the cards**: level-up cards now say what they work with ("Boosts Bard's Lute", "Fuses with War Drum"), only for things you really carry.
- **True bosses** from 26:00: tougher Drum Lord, Choir and Phantom Orchestra with extra attacks.
- **The late game**: from 15:00 the quiet parts hit less, formations grow (six new ones) and siege rings come faster in new shapes. From 27:00 a never-ending wave rolls in from the edges.
- **The Crescendo throws back every hostile note** on the screen at the horde.
- **Chests**: bigger openings for bigger chests, **Space / Enter skips** the opening, and once everything is maxed you can lock one endless card as the default for every chest.
- **Six upgrade slots** shared by upgrades and Crescendo upgrades (they all show in the row now).
- **New upgrades** in the Sanctuary: **Forager** and **Lifebloom** (more power-ups and hearts from breakables).
- **Exit to title** in the pause menu. A **loading screen** when a run starts, so the world no longer pops in.
- **Hover cards everywhere**: every gear slot and piece, and the gear you found on the death screen. **WASD** works on the cards, and **Tab** walks every page of a menu.

## Changes
- The Harp of Lines is now the **Harp of Light**: its strings land one after another, each a little dimmer, and its size is capped. Other area weapons stop growing without limit.
- The Lute Strum is now the **Bard's Lute**; the Beginner's Cape is now the **Tattered Cloak**.
- All XP orbs are the same size and play the same chime.
- Legendary gear is rarer. The last levels of each Sanctuary upgrade cost more.
- The Silencer gets faster every pass: running away no longer works forever.
- Reprieves between songs are lighter late in a run.
- The Pause menu, Sanctuary, Collection, Journal and Title screen have a new look (the Death screen and HUD come next).

## World
- Trees, stumps and rocks stay off paths. Signposts now stand only beside paths, most at forks and crossroads.
- Crates and pots are mostly by villages and camps; the wilds hold more of each biome's special breakables (beehives and friends).
- New cut tree stumps in the meadow and forest; hay bales are redrawn. The old decorative snowmen are gone (the breakable ones stay).
- The Magic Harp can turn up again if you walked away from it.

## Fixes
- Husks no longer pile up on castle ruins and walls: they walk round them.
- The title music unlocks on your first click in the browser, with a hint.
- The web build uses much less memory (the iPhone tab crash), and a note on the title screen tells us if a session ended unexpectedly.
- On the web, Esc closes menus while fullscreen instead of leaving fullscreen (Chrome and friends).
- "Your Music" is hidden on the title screen unless developer controls are on (press **`**).
- Fixed an error when the endless cards were offered, and a missing popup when a Crescendo Score was found in a chest.
- The title music and the Magic Harp's other songs (tracks 2 to 4) were missing from the web build and now play there. The Settings page no longer lets the mouse wheel turn the volume sliders, and its scroll bar stays put.
