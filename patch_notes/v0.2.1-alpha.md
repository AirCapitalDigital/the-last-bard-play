# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.2.1
*Playing-card level-ups, a camp rebuilt as a real castle, a new HUD and death screen, and a long list of keyboard, touch and polish fixes.*

## New
- **Playing-card level-ups.** Each card is a little collectible: a type ribbon, TYPE and EFFECT lines, a dry one-liner on every boost, and a footer showing what it works with. The highlighted card lifts and glows; the others are tossed aside and your pick flies to its slot with a pop. A gold star marks something you have never found before.
- **Gear from chests.** One find per chest. If you already wear something in that slot, the callout has an **Equip** button on its right; if it went on automatically it reads a greyed **Equipped**. Equipped gear flies up to the health and XP bars.
- **The Sanctuary is a castle.** Tall crenellated walls with towers, a gatehouse with an iron portcullis and a road out through the woods, flagstone courtyards and dirt paths. The camp grows from a ruin to a restored hall as you play.
- **Station names appear as you walk up**, with a golden outline round the thing you are about to use. The ruined College hall can be looked at too.
- **Fountain of Echoes** (the old Altar). Its fountain art is still to come.
- **Second Take**: a new, expensive Sanctuary upgrade, +1 level-up reroll per run (up to 10).
- **Action key.** The Crescendo key setting is now one key for everything: Crescendo, confirming in menus, picking the highlighted card, talking and opening things.
- **Subtle notes always drift round the bard** during a run, on top of the beat notes.
- **New HUD and death screen**, framed bars with the text inside, counters as "number + icon", a slimmer blue XP bar, a redder health bar that pulses when you are nearly dead (steady with Reduce flashing), and a cape gauge in the same colours.
- **Title screen**: the song's melody is written on a staff behind the title, your pet trots round the bard, and a Silencer sometimes flies over the moon.
- **Your pets roam the camp**, and chests and power-ups have their own sounds.

## Changes
- **The camp's plaza.** The Fountain of Echoes is drawn as a real fountain in the middle of the plaza and grows as you buy Sanctuary upgrades. The Armory is now the row of armor stands (the whole row lights up as one), and the Collection is a rack that fills with the weapons and boosts you have found. The bookshelf and loose chests are gone. The bard's tent has a second fire with a roast, stools, a drum and instruments, and after a run you wake up beside it.
- **The Fountain stands in the heart of the plaza**, its light rises from its top, and the wall towers sit on stone plinths with proper shadows.
- **Camp prompts float.** The black panel at the bottom is gone: the name, a one-line hint and a small "[Space] Open" key tag now float over the thing you are standing at (it still clicks and taps).
- **Husk badge.** The first foe gets its "new foe" badge on the fifth kill: they were people once, before the music and the hope went. Maestra says more about it in camp.
- Every pop-up badge now wraps all its text, so long lines no longer run off the edge. The gear-found box lines up with the three cards. The Collector and Menagerie achievements have icons in the same style as the others.
- **Halls.** The Colleges are now *halls* of the Bards College: "Restore your first hall of the Bards College". Maestra's talk is four short lines now, and she and Kage stay until they have finished speaking.
- Rally Horn is a gust of wind, not brass. Dabble the Duckling asks if you have any grapes. Rhythm Swarm is flagged as fast.
- Only the bard, Maestra Orsolya and Kage have tents in the camp (red, blue and violet). The loose armory chests are gone.
- The Resonant Stone stands in a clearing, and the ruined College waits for a *later* run than the Stone.
- Song-change badges are smaller and fit their text. The "not enough Echoes" note sits beside the last-run line in orange.

## Controls and accessibility
- **Tabs show two colours:** the tab that is open keeps its gold border, the one under your cursor or keyboard gets a sky-blue one.
- **Menus walk the layout.** Arrows and WASD move along the row or down the column, stop at the edges and no longer wrap to the tab bar. Only **Up** reaches the tabs; Down goes through a page's sub-tabs first, then the page. Lists scroll all the way before leaving.
- **Esc and Settings work over the level-up cards** and take you back to them.
- **Cards**: Left and Right step along the three cards and stop at the ends; the number hints are subtle and hidden on touch; a mouse resting on one card no longer lights it while the keys move on.
- **Every hover icon is reachable by keyboard**: the pause-menu build page, the death screen, and the Collection and Journal tiles.
- **Touch**: drag anywhere in a menu to scroll, even over buttons.

## Fixes
- **The browser build uses much less video memory** (the world's props are packed far tighter, 70 MB less), and it picks a quality level for your device: Low, Medium or High (Settings > Quality, reload after changing). Phones and older tablets load less of the world at once and show fewer foes at a time.
- A bard who never picks up their orbs could slow the game down over ten minutes. When too many pile up, the orbs now pool into bigger, brighter ones (up to a big white-red orb for 100 XP or more) that you still have to collect.
- A key press that fired the Crescendo as a level-up appeared could also pick a card. Cards now ignore input for a third of a second after they appear.
- The camp's ruined towers no longer stand outside the walls, and the wall ring and towers meet cleanly.
- The camp's corner and buttress towers were never drawn; they are now, and the gate towers are no longer cut off.
- You can no longer walk into or through the camp walls.
- Note trails to a find no longer draw over the cards.
- On a fresh save the pause menu's Resume and Exit buttons can be reached with the keys.
- Stats page spacing is even; the Collection and Journal borders are complete.
- **Reduce flashing** also turns off the card and equip animations; the setting now says so.

## Notes batch (later additions)
- **Mini elites**: every 20 to 40 seconds a bigger, gold-ringed foe arrives with four times the health, a harder bite, four times the XP and a guaranteed chest.
- **Second Verse**: a new boost, +1 projectile for every weapon that throws or looses them (up to 5). Level-up cards now say **+1 PROJECTILE** when the next level adds one.
- 37 new achievements with silly titles (kills, crates, deaths, hours played, full gear sets by rarity, every pet, weapon, boost and foe) and four new Stats cards.
- Later in a run a strong bass drum can draw a small geometric formation of hex sigils, like the snare's, instead of a pair of foes.
- Camp: nothing is selected when you arrive, and the bard's floating notes play in camp too.
- Balance: slowed foes move at 62% (was 50%), Rally Horn pushes a little less and a little slower, Mending gives less.
- Ruins are themed per biome; houses and tents are faded with dark windows; a brown road runs from the camp gate; the Magic Harp and Resonant Stone have a ringed clearing; LEVEL UP is easier to read; the death screen lists every damage source; locked entries show a padlock.
