# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.3.0
*A Steam Deck that actually fits and names its own buttons, settings in proper tabs, a grander Fountain of Echoes, a drunken new friend, evolutions that finally look the part, a picture for every piece of gear, and a late game that bites back.*

Big one this time. A lot of this came straight from your feedback and from me sitting with a Deck in my hands for a few days. Thank you to everyone who sent notes, screenshots and logs. Keep them coming, they really do end up in the game.

## New
- **A drunken monkey pet.** **The Drunken Monkey** carries a cymbal in each hand and gives **+1 cymbal** to Cymbal Crash and its evolutions. Survive 30 minutes in one run with Cymbal Crash at max level (or evolved) to win him over. He is rumoured to throw a party in camp.
- **The Fountain of Echoes grows through six stages** (one more bird perches on its rim at each stage), from a bird bath with a sparrow on the rim to a grand fountain with a pillar of golden light, spiralling jets and drifting orbs. The **golden glow on the floor** round the bard now belongs to the Fountain: none on a fresh bard, noticeable half-way, the full floor at the end.
- **A picture for every piece of gear.** All 97 pieces have their own drawn icon: hoods, vests, boots, cloaks, relics and charms (some cloaks used to be boots). **Headwear is shown as worn:** hoods wrap the bard's head, caps and hats sit on it, crowns stand above it, a helm covers the face and a laurel rings it, in the camp, in a run and on the loadout bard.
- **Evolutions look the part.** Each twin evolution now hits at full strength, fires 12% faster, reaches a little further and, when both halves strike together, rings out in a flourish of its own colour and sparkle (every fifth cast for the single-weapon evolutions). The other evolutions hit 10% harder.

## Steam Deck and controllers
- **Fits the Deck's screen:** fullscreen with no black bars on a 16:10 display.
- **Your pad's own button names:** hints name the buttons of an Xbox, PlayStation, Switch or Steam Deck pad (A / Cross / B / A, L1, R2 and so on). Pick one yourself in Settings > Controls.
- **A quicker walk in the camp** (a flat speed, 30% above the starting one, whatever gear you wear; pets and companions keep up).
- **A party hat for the Drunken Monkey,** and the bard wears one too while the monkey is along. It only changes how the bard looks: whatever you have equipped stays worn with its stats, and a small violet sparkle badge on that piece in the loadout, the pause menu and the Collection says its look is being replaced.
- **The Ballad** shows its notes on the loadout and pause-menu bard, and the bard holds nothing (and nothing glows in the hands) while it is the starting weapon.
- **The light above the Fountain of Echoes** is always there, a gentle column that grows taller with the fountain, and a tall, bright beam with a bright core and a ring round its foot when you can afford an upgrade.
- **Show the frame rate** (Settings > Graphics) puts a small readout in the corner.
- **Down and Up walk every row** of every menu, sliders and switches included: a menu never jumps over a row any more (a pad's d-pad used to skip some).
- **Linked tiles stay in view:** in the Unlocks page, a weapon's fusions that are below the fold hop up to its edge, held by their strings, and settle back as you scroll.

## Settings
- **Five tabs:** Graphics, Gameplay, Controls, Audio and General. LB / RB flip through them.
- **New options:** fullscreen, V-Sync, a frame-rate limit, a Controls page that lists the pad's buttons, and a switch for showing on Discord that you are playing.

## Changes
- **Late game: the crowd is back.** A full 30 minute run with max gear on the Deck showed way too few foes at the end, so waves grow faster late, enemies get tougher a little sooner, and the echo bosses have twice the health. If you were bored at minute 28, that should be fixed. Tell me if it is now too much.
- **Magnet Crescendo** deals no damage: it only nudges the crowd back, and the pull on orbs and Echoes is the point.
- **Starting weapons** are the original bard set again: lute, pan flute, war drum, tambourine, rally horn, harp of light and ballad. The bard in the loadout and the pause menu holds the one you chose (the title screen bard keeps the lute).
- **Takashi's Ocarina:** the Ocarina is renamed in honour of a playtester.
- **The Crescendo button** has a bigger note, glowing rings and a slow pulse while it is ready (phone and desktop).
- **Companion boosts** wear a small sword, bow or flame in the corner of their card and tile.
- **The songs you always have** with the Magic Harp are now **Lonely Espresso** and **Stompwood Run** (with the Overture), listed first. Hearthside Reel and Greywall Hymn are found as torn sheet music like the others.
- **Crescendo cards** are aqua (gold was too close to the String family), show the Crescendo's own mark (a note in swelling rings) with a small symbol for what each upgrade does, and the Wider Chord icon no longer looks like a Wi-Fi sign.
- **Cape gauge** (Settings > Gameplay) shows a small picture of the cape filling for each choice.
- **The golden light above the Fountain of Echoes** (when you can afford an upgrade) now reaches all the way down to the fountain's foot, fading out near it, so it never stops short on the small fountains.
- **The pet you wear follows you round the camp.** Mote the Orb has a new line.
- **The companion offer card** no longer talks about opening a College or weapons (those are already yours from meeting them); it only says who will fight beside you in this run. Foes that fall during a companion's meeting drop no XP, Echoes or loot.
- **Companions and Maestra guard you properly while they talk.** It was too weak to even notice before. Now: a glowing ward ring round them, a pulse that rolls out every second and wipes the ordinary foes it crosses, and a barrage of their own weapon (Kage's whirling axes, Rowan's fans of arrows, Morwen's bursting orbs, Maestra's homing notes).
- **The Closer has a lot more health**, and the message at 30 minutes now reads "IT GETS QUIET... TOO QUIET...". Sounds about right.
- **The Closer now has a health bar,** so you can see how the fight is going.
- **Quick Fingers** says what it does: weapons attack 12% faster per level.
- **Gear popups** use a red minus for a stat the swap would drop, instead of a red X.
- **The death screen's Return to camp button** glows like the other go buttons.
- **Small text is a little larger** (nothing below 12 px except the XP bar's label).

## Fixes
- On the Deck, the d-pad and stick could stop moving between cards on the second set of treasure cards. Fixed (tell me if you still catch it).
- The title screen no longer shows a strip of the game under it on 16:10 screens.
- Menus no longer double-step or skip rows with a controller, and dropdowns are plain buttons you can cycle with left and right.
