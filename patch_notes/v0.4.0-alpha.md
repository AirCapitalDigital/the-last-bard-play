# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.4.0
*Make your bard yours: a Mirror to change skin, hair, eyes and more, a Fountain that grows right in front of you, a Crescendo button you can read at a glance, and a long list of fixes from your notes.*

Quick one after 0.3, and a fun one. A lot of this came straight from your feedback again. Thank you, keep it coming.

## New
- **The Mirror.** Your bard finally gets to look like *your* bard. Hit **CUSTOMIZE** under the bard on the title screen, or walk up to the standing mirror in the corner of camp by the tent and the wall tower. Pick **skin, hair, eyes, belt colour and hairstyle**, with a live preview that follows you everywhere the bard is drawn: runs, camp, loadout, pause menu and title.
- **Wild looks are won, not bought.** Rainbow hair, flame hair, starlight hair, ghost skin, glowing eyes, spiky "Wild" hair and more are unlocked by achievements you already know (find every pet, reach level 50, survive 30 minutes...). Hover a locked swatch to see how to win it.
- **The Fountain of Echoes grows right in front of you.** Spend your Echoes, close the menu, and the fountain climbs through every stage you just bought, one at a time, each with a ring of light, sparks and a chime. Each stage also adds a bird on the rim now, up to six.
- **Something is lighting the way.** If you wander near a lost Magic Harp, you might notice a few small violet lanterns scattered about. No arrow, no trail. Just a reason to go and look.
- **Show the bard's helmet** (Settings > Gameplay): turn it off and no headwear is drawn anywhere. Your helmet stays equipped and keeps its stats.

## Changes
- **The Crescendo button is readable from the corner of your eye.** The wait now sits big over the note, disappears the moment it is ready, and the button glows harder with a ring that swells out. Its colour follows the Crescendo you have equipped, on desktop and phone, and the wave's inner ring takes the colour too.
- **Health and XP bars react.** A heart or health bonus sweeps a bright band along the HP bar, and an XP magnet (power-up or Crescendo) does the same on the XP bar. When your health gets low the screen edges throb red and the bar throbs harder the lower you go (steady with Reduce flashing).
- **Music follows the moment.** It rises slowly instead of jumping at the start of a run. When the bard falls, the song slows and sinks and the camera leans in, and it stays that way until you are back in camp, where it winds back up.
- **A chest holds one find.** Never two gear pieces or a piece and a Crescendo any more, plus at most one sheet of music. It keeps the chest screen tidy.
- **Maestra leaves before the College callout,** and her guard stands down. The "College selected" line now waits until the field is clear.
- **The armory's relic stand** is closer to the mannequin and shows the relic's own picture circling the plinth. The relic circling the bard on the menu avatars is its picture too.
- **The upgrades page fits on one screen** with no cut-off rows, and shows how many Echoes you have spent next to REFUND ALL. The Echoes counters have the Echoes icon.
- **Achievement badges** wait for the camp's own line under the timer before popping up.
- **Cleaner camp and world:** the crypt floor lost its odd purple rune marks, haybales and barrels keep a tile away from houses, and the beehives, crates and pots that were left standing after a run no longer follow you into the Sanctuary.

## Fixes
- Maestra's list of questions no longer closes the whole chat when you press Enter, Space, E or the pad's A. The first option is highlighted so Up, Down and Enter work.
- CUSTOMIZE on the title screen can be reached with the keyboard and the pad.
- The Muted Crescendo's text is short and simple everywhere it is shown.
