# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.4.1
*Bosses with weaknesses, and a pile of fixes from your notes.*

Short and sweet this time. The big new thing is that some of the nastiest foes now shrug off certain weapons, so what you carry matters.

## New
- **Immunities and resistances.** Bosses and the Bone Amalgam are no longer equally squishy to everything. A small tag rises off them in the weapon family's colour (with its little symbol) when your hit is blocked.
  - **The Drum Lord** is immune to percussion and resists blades (half damage).
  - **The Choir** is immune to voice and resists spells (35% less).
  - **The Phantom Orchestra** is immune to strings and resists bows (25% less).
  - **The Bone Amalgam** is immune to blades.
  - Companions, the Crescendo and damage over time always land, and wind weapons are never blocked. If everything you carry is immune to a boss, it still takes a quarter damage, so no build gets stuck. The arrival message and the Compendium tell you what each one is immune to.

## Changes
- **Revive comes first.** On the death screen the highlighted button is Revive whenever you have one, so a click a moment too early cannot send you back to camp.
- **Pets show up on the death screen.** A pet you win mid-run (the Drunken Monkey, for example) is now listed under "New in your collection".
- **Carried boosts keep coming.** A boost you already carry stays on offer until it is maxed, even if the weapon or companion that unlocked it is gone.
- The achievement for trying every boost now says "boost card".

## Fixes
- A click on the left arrow of a "<  value  >" setting now goes back (it always went forward, so songs and other lists seemed shuffled).
- Fixed a stuck key: if a menu closed while you were still holding S (or any of W, A, D, or the stick), the bard could keep walking on its own after returning to camp.
