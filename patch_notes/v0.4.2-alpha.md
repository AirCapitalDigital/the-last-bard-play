# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.4.2
*Max Fountain players asked for a real fight. Here it is.*

Short one, and a spicy one. A lot of you have the Fountain fully grown and were bored by the late game, so the Fountain now pays you back with a much bigger horde.

## Changes
- **A grown Fountain brings a bigger horde.** The more of the Fountain of Echoes you own, the more foes turn up: about 60% more at half the Fountain, and **3.5 times as many** with all of it. It grows gently at first and ramps up near the top, and it applies to the beat waves, the steady trickle and the big surges alike.
- If this feels like too much (or not enough), tell me. It is a single number I can turn.
