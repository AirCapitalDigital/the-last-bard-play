# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.4.5
*The Parched Reach opens, a choir of wailers sings together, and loot gets harder to come by.*

## New
- **A desert biome: the Parched Reach.** Sand, pale dunes, cracked clay and the odd small oasis, with cacti, barrel cacti, dry brush, tumbleweeds, sandstone buttes, obelisks, broken columns and bleached ribs. Palms stand only in the oases and reeds grow only at the water. No villages here: only abandoned camps with cold fire rings, sandstone tower ruins and the occasional palm grove. Its own breakable, the **Desert Amphora**, sits beside the crates and pots.
- **Igloos** in the snow, scattered about and as the main buildings of the snow settlements. The forest now has cabins and trapper camps instead of full villages.
- **The Wailer choir.** From about 6:30 a ring of wailers lands round you and sings together: sometimes all in one volley, sometimes as a turning wave so the notes spiral in. It grows by a singer every 40 seconds.
- **A choice when every card is taken.** The first time everything is at its top level, a screen asks what your level-ups should give from now on: **Endless Boosts** (for the leaderboards: the three endless cards, forever) or **Loot Mode** (for the treasure hunter: level-ups stop pausing the game, pay Echoes, may drop a small chest, and your chests hold 30% more gear). Change it any time in Settings > Gameplay.
- **Power-up callouts.** A bouncy line over the bard says what a power-up just did ("HUSH: the horde freezes for 5 s"). On by default, with a switch in Settings > Gameplay.
- **The Crescendo button shows in the camp**, with no cooldown there, and its colour follows the rarity of the score you wear (a Perfected one is orange). The Crescendo itself now sounds like one: a chord swelling to a boom.
- **Restore defaults** at the foot of every Settings page.

## Changes
- **Loot is scarcer.** Chests hold gear far less often (bronze 12%, silver 22%, gold 38%, boss 55%), and rarity now follows the run: rare from about 3:00, epic from 8:00, legendary from 15:00, mythic from 30:00. Commons and uncommon fill first; the top rarities take many runs. Anything can still drop at any time, just rarely.
- **Quiet parts of a song** thin the horde by at most 20% from about 7:00 on (early on they stay a real breather).
- **Staccato** foes now tense at their own distances and rest for different times, so a group never lunges together and the red lane ends near you; golden ones dash farther. **Bass Behemoths** are tougher. Gold foes climb to five stars.
- **Phantom Crescendo:** about a quarter of the foes near the phantom walk toward it instead of you.
- **The talking wards** (Maestra, Kage, Rowan, Morwen): silent now, softer to look at, fewer projectiles that each hit harder, and whoever was talking leaves in a flash of light at the same moment as their ward.
- **Tougher early game and bosses:** more wailers and discords as a run goes on, golems arrive sooner, and the Choir fires three aimed waves and has more health.
- The **Fountain** on the Upgrades page is now the Fountain icon and "Lv. 1/6" (the bird bath is level 1). The floor glow is softer.
- Boost tags (DMG, CRT, SPD...) are three letters at most and sit at the icon's corner. The **Wider Chord** has a new picture, and the **Final Flourish** note is bigger and easier to see.
- The **Mirror** says how a look was unlocked. Pet pop-ups no longer mention the tent.
- The Leaderboard shows what ended a run (a skull) and how grown the Fountain was.

## Fixes
- Gold foes' stars sat far above their heads and foes were drawn slowly after the pumpkin Husk was added: fixed.
- On touch screens, tapping an icon on the death screen, the pause menu or the Leaderboard opens its card and tapping it again closes it; the Return to camp button no longer flashes, and cards no longer stay on screen after a menu closes.
- The Leaderboard no longer reports a Crescendo before you have found it.
- Boss arrival messages no longer give away which weapon families they resist before you can use them.
