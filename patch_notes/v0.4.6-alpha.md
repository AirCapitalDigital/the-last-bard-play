# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.4.6
*Fire catches, hats cover heads, the camp learns to behave with a controller, and the new foes get their teeth.*

## New
- **Fire is visible.** Some of the foes hit by the Ember Bolt (and anything fused from it), the mage companion or the Infernal Crescendo now catch light for a moment, with little flames flickering over their shoulders.
- **The chest opening shows what you won.** Each pick rises out of the chest as a small flared stack of three treasure cards instead of a glowing orb: a silver chest shows two stacks, a gold one three.
- **New sounds.** A coin ping when you buy something with Echoes (and a falling ping when you take a level back), a soft clunk and chime when you equip gear, a pet, a College, a Crescendo score or a look, and a gentle chime when the Crescendo has recharged.
- **Arrows for every trail of notes.** The chevron at the bard's feet that leads you to the cape's Husk now also points along each trail of notes, in the trail's own colour: Kage's red, the Resonant Stone's violet, the ruined College's gold, the ranger's green, the mage's blue.
- **NEW tags in the Mirror.** The looks and vanity you have just unlocked wear a gold NEW tag the next time you open it, so the "!" over the Mirror tells you what it was for.
- **The Artifacts tab is an inventory**, like the Collection: a tile for each artifact and a panel with its story. The little pedestals round the Fountain are gone.

## Changes
- **Headgear covers the head.** Hoods, coifs and helms now hide the hair under them, and caps, berets and straw hats hide the top of it, on the bard in the run and on the avatar in the menus.
- **The Lute Goblin is easier to catch.** A pulsing ring (gold while it hunts, red once it runs and can be hurt) and sparkles mark it, and it leaves a trail of fading coins and orbs (not real loot) behind it. It stays near the screen for at least 16 seconds before it may run, and runs a little slower.
- **Foes you could not read now show their hand.** The **Scarecrow Choir** never moves: it stands on its stake and sings at you from almost off the screen. The **Bone Cantor** stands still and chants, with a purple ring and "ARISE!", before its skeletons climb out, and is tougher. The **Sand Burrower** starts its attack from farther away, tunnels faster and cannot be hurt while it tunnels, marks a bigger ring where you are heading, shouts "ERUPTS!", then stays exposed a moment longer. The **Meadow Goblin** throws flaming daggers.
- **Bosses, new foes and choirs keep their distance from each other.** A boss's arrival, a new foe's first showing and a Wailer choir are now kept 30 to 45 seconds apart, so the pop-ups never pile on.
- **Fewer villages and tents in the meadow**, and the hedgehog's chances are doubled to make up for it, so he is still easy to find.
- The screen-clearing power-up is now called the **Sun Burst**.
- **The Horde-Breaker** achievement (and the Flame hair it unlocks) now asks for 20,000 foes in a single run.
- The camp's **Crescendo** has a short 3-second cooldown again, so it cannot flood the courtyard.
- The Leaderboard opens on **All versions**, so a new version never looks like a reset.

## Controller and menus
- Up and Down in a menu now stay in their own column or grid until it runs out, so a button beside a list is never the "next row": no more skips to the Headgear button in the Mirror, or sideways on the death screen.
- After equipping something in the Collection, the selection stays on that tile instead of jumping to the top.
- Settings: Down from the last row now reaches Resume, Exit to title and Quit game.
- The pause menu opens with Resume selected.
- In the camp, A no longer fires the Crescendo (R2 does), and the SET OUT, MENU and mute buttons hide while you use a controller.
- The key hints no longer flicker between keyboard and controller names (E and R2).
- The bard turns smoothly through its facings instead of popping.

## Fixes
- Maestra no longer vanishes a couple of seconds before her last line and her ward end: she leaves in a flash of light at the same moment as her ward.
- The tip about the final boss now names the Closer.
