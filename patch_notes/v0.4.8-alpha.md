# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.4.8
*A willow with a secret, a desert and a graveyard full of new foes, Wild Magic that looks the part, and boosts that show who they help.*

## New
- **An Echo Tree.** Now and then, out in the world, a strange willow stands by a pond in a glade of its own (never in the meadow, and not until you have a few runs behind you). Walk up to it and see what it gives you. Back at camp there is an empty planter in the top-left corner, with a pond beside it. The tree grows with every run you finish and the time you play, over six stages, and after each run it ripens a harvest of Echoes that grows with it. Its echoes are bigger now and sparkle, and Settings > Gameplay lets you pick the tree's colours (camp, meadow, forest, snow, crypt or desert).
- **New desert foes.** The **Mummy** (a sturdier Husk), the **Undead Pharaoh** (keeps its distance, throws sand bolts and now and then calls for locusts, shown with a purple ring and "LOCUSTS!") and the **Locust Swarm** (a fast, fragile cloud that also turns up on its own). The Sand Burrower is capped at three at a time.
- **New foes in the snow, the forest and the crypt.** The **Yeti** throws snowballs, the **Ent** slams the ground and its roots can hold you in place (a dash tears you free, and fire hurts it three times as much), **Grave Ghosts** drift in as a pack of ten or so, and the **Graveyard Abomination** sprays a fan of ichor. The Yeti, Ent, Abomination, Pharaoh and Bone Amalgam cannot be knocked back. Every one of them has a keepsake to find.
- **Wild Magic looks the part.** A wildfire leaves a handful of small flame traps that burn foes (and you), a storm sends three waves of crackling lightning and leaves scorch marks, and meteors, frost, gales, backfires and frostbite all got proper visuals. The pop-up lines are wider, so nothing is cut off.
- **Three more Lute evolutions**, for the weapons that had none: **Charmer's Lute** (Lute + Serpent), **Thunder Biwa** (Lute + Taiko) and **Bard's Spearsong** (Lute + Atlatl).
- **Boosts that help everything show it.** Quick Fingers, Wider Chord, Brutal Verse and Second Verse (for projectile weapons) now send a blue string with a small "+" travelling down it to every weapon they help, in the HUD and the pause menu. Boost strings into an evolution run rainbow and into a fusion the fusion's blue.
- **Boosts reach fusions and evolutions.** A family boost counts for every family in the weapon: damage multiplies, crit adds up, and carrying the weapon makes those boosts show up in your offers.
- **A what's new panel** on the title screen, top right, for bards who have played before and missed an update. "Got it" or Play puts it away.
- **Bosses are easy to find.** A boss in view wears a pulsing red and gold ring, a red glow and a chevron. Off screen, an arrow at the edge shows where it is and how far. The boss health bars are gone.
- **Every Crescendo says it throws hostile notes back,** and the Crescendo of the First Note also pulls in every orb and Echo, like the Magnet Crescendo.
- **The camp.** The Resonant Stone stands on a flagstone plaza with a brazier at each corner, Maestra Orsolya and the Journal have moved, and the Journal's glow now sits on its own pedestal.

## Changes
- **Enemies get tougher a little faster, all through the run:** about 10% more health at 10 minutes and 20% more at 24. Health still grows smoothly, minute by minute. **Mini bosses and echo bosses toughen too**, at half the pace of other foes. The Drum Lord, Choir and Phantom Orchestra also start with 25% more health.
- **Late runs get big a little earlier:** the plain Husks come fewer and bigger from 13 minutes, not 15.
- **Chests give a little less** between 5 and 15 minutes, and levels 11 to 20 cost a little more.
- **The Closer has less health again** (30% less), and the text in its closing scenes fits on screen.
- **IMMUNE and RESIST pops** show at most once a second, so a boss is readable while you hit it.
- **The power-up vignette** (the ice blue and the gold) is 20% lighter, so it does not swamp a busy screen.
- **Locust swarms are easy to see:** twice the locusts, bigger, outlined and brighter, with a faint haze behind them. Rowan has a long red braid out of her hood and both eyes.
- **Base weapons wear their family's colour** on cards, HUD, pause menu and effects, and the boosts match: family boosts in their family's hue, every other boost in the same blue (Wider Chord used to be purple on its icon and blue on its card).
- **Weapon cards list every fusion and evolution partner** once you have made that recipe, "Evolves with" for evolutions and "Fuses with" for fusions, on both cards of the pair. Rerolls also work on the treasure cards from chests.
- **The Monsters page:** a foe's keepsake shows the kill it dropped at, the stats are coloured cards with little icons, and the page is tighter so far fewer of them need a scroll bar (the right stick scrolls the rest). The Echo Seed's text says whether you planted it.
- **Collection:** a fusion says "fused from" its two parts and an evolution "evolved from".
- **The Steam Machine** is its own device on the leaderboard (the Deck keeps its icon). The Leaderboard opens on Records > Longest run every time, and the run icons can be reached with the d-pad, in fixed slots so every row lines up.
- **Smoother walking on screens that are not 60 Hz.** The bard's steps follow your screen's real refresh rate and frame cap (a 90 Hz OLED Deck, a 40 or 30 fps mode) and keep doing so through dips.
- **Pause menu loadout:** no more "Walk alone" or "No pet" in the middle of a run (you can only switch).

## Controller and menus
- On the title screen, Right from any entry reaches "Got it" and Left returns to Play.
- On the Leaderboard, the d-pad steps between a run's icons, and the card opens on the one you are on.
- Hovering "Nothing" in a gear list and then moving the mouse hides its card again.

## Fixes
- The card scene could push the Reroll button far down or off the screen when the cards were stretched. It now shrinks to fit.
- A "walk blocked?" message with a lot of text could pop up in the game after being rooted. It is gone for players.
- The fusion and evolution borders on the left-most tiles were clipped. They sit inside the tile now.
- The "-" button on the Upgrades page sat a little above the price button. It is level now.
- Steam Machine runs were reported as Steam Deck runs.
