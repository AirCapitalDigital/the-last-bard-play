# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.12.10

## The Crucible
- **An all-new bestiary:** every foe in the Crucible is now a creature of the volcano, with its own attacks, and each Movement has a new champion. Each Movement teaches something you will need at the top.
- **A pedestal starts the climb:** walk the arena first, then step up to the pedestal to choose any Ember weapon you have found (and a boost, with the Booster Pack).
- **Rewards:** a Cards reward is one card, every time; a Crescendo reward lets you choose between three; the reward tablets have their own colours.
- **Nothing useless inside:** no cards or gear that only help with XP, pickup range or drop luck.
- **Flawless achievements** now count boss waves and a flawless climb, not ordinary waves.

## Fixes
- The Crescendo button stays hidden in the Crucible until you have one.
