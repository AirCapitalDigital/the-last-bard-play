# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.12.11

## The Crucible
- **More faces in the crowd:** every Crucible creature now comes in variations, and each champion has a second look.
- **New reward chests:** a volcanic reliquary after an ordinary wave, a champion's hoard after a champion, with a clearer arrow over each.
- **A walk before the climb:** you arrive at the edge of the arena and a trail of notes leads you to the pedestal.
- **The lava fights back:** something lives in the lava ring now. Find a pillar.
- **Flawless** now only counts on a champion's wave.

## Fixes
- Coming from Ember Island, the Crucible's creatures could appear as old Husks.
- Reward card names and tags fit their cards.
- On Ember Island you arrive up on the beach instead of beside Jad's boat.
