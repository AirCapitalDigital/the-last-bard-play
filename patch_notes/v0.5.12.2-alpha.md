# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.12.2
*A fairer leaderboard, a friendlier Andy, and fixes from the first climbs of the Crucible.*

## Changes
- **Most bosses counts every boss you fell.** Each boss in a group and every boss that turns up late in a long run now counts on the leaderboard. Before, a run topped out at about four however many you beat.
- **Longest run is fairer.** Past the half-hour mark, the clock only counts the time you truly survive.
- **The Crucible only offers weapons you have earned:** the Ember versions of weapons you have found out in the world, and Andy's hunter weapons once you have bought them (they can now be your starting weapon there too). Fusions and evolutions can still come up whatever you have found, and a first one joins your Collection.
- **The Cape of Embers burns roots away** before they can hold you.
- **Andy calls you by your rank,** not always "whelp", and his hunt board shows all three hunts in a row with a clearer line about the Marks you can spend.

## Fixes
- The Wolf Pelt Cloak, Royal Cape, Ghostly Shroud and Smouldering Cape no longer show as plain red capes: each keeps its own look, with the cape's gauge as a band along the hem.
- The Sapling Pack sits on the middle of your back, its leaves above your head, instead of floating beside you when you turn.
