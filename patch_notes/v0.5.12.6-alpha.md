# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.12.6
*Crucible fixes, a Power-ups page, and a stronger Phantom Meowstro.*

## Changes
- **Power-ups have their own page in the Journal,** after the Bard's College, instead of the end of the Foes list.
- **The trio's two forms are both in your Collection** once the Tablet is read: wear the broken piece for its old weakness or the whole one for its gifts, no switch needed.
- **The Phantom Meowstro is far stronger** (it asks for every other pet): 8% more damage, 4% crit chance, 4% move speed and 5% more health.
- **The Crucible's three pets stay hidden** until Ember Island is open.

## Fixes
- **Your best Crucible wave and every Crucible counter are kept** when the game restarts. They were lost on every restart, and with them the Board of Records' marks and progress toward the Crucible's achievements.
- **The Crucible's pets are earned in the Crucible only.** Any run could hand all three out; they are taken back unless your best climb earned them.
- The tip of Jad's hood is no longer cut off.
