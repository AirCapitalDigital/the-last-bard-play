# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.12.7
*The Wind Harp plays for Ember Island.*

## New
- **The Wind Harp on Ember Island's beach** works like the Magic Harp in the Sanctuary: pick any of the Crucible's songs and it plays at once. When it ends, the island picks the next one, as before. Tharrun's own song joins the list once you have beaten him.
