# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.12.8

## Changes
- **The Crucible's entrance keeps its secrets:** a shorter set of pages about surviving the waves, and the Echoes you earn inside are yours to keep.
- **The Board of Records** only seems to keep count of something until you clear your first wave.

## Fixes
- **Tapping a highlighted place on a phone uses it,** in the Sanctuary and on Ember Island (only its button worked).
- **Entering the Crucible no longer freezes behind an empty screen;** if its weapon pick ever fails to show, it comes back by itself.
