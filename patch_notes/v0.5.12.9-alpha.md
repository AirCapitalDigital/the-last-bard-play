# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.12.9

## The Crucible
- **A reward chest after every wave:** it rises in the middle of the arena under a pillar of light, fuller after a flawless wave, grander after a boss. Walk to it to choose your reward.
- **Reward cards have their own look,** so they are never picked out of habit.
- **No XP orbs or chests inside:** everything comes from the reward after each wave (Cards now gives more picks).
- **You go in bare:** your usual look and gear stay outside, and gear found inside now shows on the bard and in the pause menu.
- **Lava sets you alight:** a burning health bar with a countdown, and flames on the bard.
- **The lava throws rocks** that crash down and leave pools, and **spits fire** you can hide from behind a pillar.
- **Ember-forged foes** crawl out of the lava. Lava mends them, but Ember weapons still burn them. Bellows Gust now sets them alight.

## Changes
- **Molten synergy strings** with the Ember skin, in the HUD and the pause menu.
