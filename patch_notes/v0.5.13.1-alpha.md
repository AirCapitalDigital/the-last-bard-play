# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.13.1

## New
- **Foes left and the climb's clock.** In the Crucible the kill counter shows how many foes are left in the wave, and the wave banner shows how long your climb has taken.
- **Climb time at the end.** A climb's end screen shows your climb time and the wave you reached, and your fastest clear is kept on the Board of Records.
- **Boss names.** On a boss wave each champion wears its name over its head.

## Changes
- **Conduits read clearly:** a thick red tether streams life into the boss, and the boss glows as it mends. Conduits and pylons no longer keep a wave going once every foe is down.
- **Pylons show their shield:** a gold dome, with threads and a small shield over every foe it guards.
- **Charges look different:** the Obsidian Ram's and the Caldera Knight's charge warning is a dark crimson lane with racing arrows, unlike the Kiln Toad's line of fire.
- **Supplies always offer a heal** when you are hurt.
- **After the Cooling Rain** the lava heats back up over a few seconds instead of flashing back.
- **The Bellows Gust** pushes foes further and stuns them.
- **A pet earned in the Crucible** is greeted on Ember Island after the climb, not in the middle of a fight.
- No magnet power-up in the Crucible (nothing drops there for it to pull).
- The melted bells in the arena are gone (they looked like conduits).
- **Evolutions say so:** in the Unlocks lists an evolution reads "evolution" and "evolves with it" in rainbow letters.
- **Ember Island's beach** is closed at both ends by walls of basalt instead of an invisible edge.

## Fixes
- The Magma Wyrmling breathes exactly where it warned.
- Lava bubbles no longer pop up through the bridges.
