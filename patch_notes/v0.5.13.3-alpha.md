# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.13.3

## New
- **Every song loops seamlessly,** and when the music changes the next song now crossfades in instead of one ending and the next starting cold. Settings > Audio has a new **Repeat the current song** switch.
- **Tharrun has a Journal page** (once you have met him), and the Crucible's foes and power-ups each have their own Crucible section.
- **Zoom on Ember Island,** as in the Sanctuary (the wheel, a trackpad or a two-finger pinch).
- **Ember Island's far side:** the land beyond the canyon walls is now a wild volcano flank, with ash woods, rocks, cinder cones, lava pools and the chasm's lava running out to the ridges.

## Changes
- **The Crucible is a climb of base weapons:** no fusions or evolutions inside, and once every weapon and boost is maxed the Cards reward is no longer offered.
- **Tharrun:** his parts have less health, the camera no longer slides under you during the fight, and phones no longer zoom out.
- **The Smoke Mother's breath** is a smoky warning followed by a gout of rolling fire.
- **The Kiln Toad's beam** leaves a trail of flames, and the Obsidian Ram's warning arrows move slower.
- Conduits and pylons are never gold, and no Magnet Crescendo is offered in the Crucible.
- A Crucible foe's Journal card says the wave it joins the climb.

## Fixes
- A foe could appear in the arena during the reward and the countdown.
- A short stutter at the start of a Crucible wave on phones.
- Props like an open coffin could stand over a house.
- The Journal's Foes tab and Maestra could keep a "!" that never went away.
