# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.13.4

## New
- **Ember Island comes alive:** ember crabs scuttle on the black sand, ravens take off as you pass, gulls glide over the sea, lava salamanders slip away into the ash woods, and now and then a faint ashfall drifts by.
- **Jad's Harp:** the song harp is now Jad's own, a fine obsidian-and-ember harp beside his bedroll.
- **The Tally Stone:** the Board of Records is now an ancient carved stone, its 63 marks glowing as you reach each wave.
- **Jad's hearth:** the odd shrine behind his tent is now a proper hearth with his kettle and offerings.
- **Skip the Crucible's rules:** a "Don't show this again" box takes you straight in from the gate; Jad can show the rules again any time.
- **Back out of the Ember weapon pick** to walk about before you start.

## Changes
- The Crucible is a trial in the caldera, not a climb: its wording everywhere now says so.
- Jad's Movements banner stands behind him instead of hiding him, and the head on a pike is gone from his camp.
- The pill and name over a place on Ember Island stay over it instead of jumping onto it.
- The near pillars of the chasm bridge no longer fade.
