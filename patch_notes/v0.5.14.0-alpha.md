# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.14.0 "Forged in Fire"
*The Crucible gets its own gear, a victory worth the fight, and fiercer fire everywhere.*

## New
- **Three Crucible gear sets.** Inside the Crucible, gear now comes only from its own three sets, each with bonuses at 2, 4 and 6 pieces worn:
  - **Emberforged** (burn): set foes alight far more often, burning foes take more damage, and at six they erupt in flame when they die.
  - **Obsidian** (defence): take less damage, a Glass Shell turns a blow aside, and at six the shell shatters hostile shots and bursts into cutting shards.
  - **Ashwalker** (speed): move faster, slip away untouchable after a hit, and at six you leave drifting ash that slows and weakens foes.
  - Every piece also carries a little fire resistance. Mix two sets or chase a full six: the gear cards show how many you wear and what the next bonus does.
  - **Clear the Crucible once** and the sets' looks unlock as vanity to wear anywhere.
- **Tharrun's fall is a moment.** The clock stops the instant he falls, a cutscene raises a dais in the middle of the caldera with the Cape of Embers and the Crescendo of Embers floating over it, and you walk up to claim them. Then an exit beacon waits for you to leave when you are ready.
- **A bonus reward for a flawless champion's wave:** after your reward, a second one.
- **The Pyre Warden swings his halberd for real,** and has a new attack: the Pyre Brand, a line of roaring fire pillars racing toward you.
- **Obsidian Halo,** a new Crucible power-up in place of the Bellows Gust: eight shards of black glass circle you, cutting foes and shattering hostile shots.
- **Bare to the Bone:** a hidden achievement and the title The Unclad, for clearing the Crucible without ever choosing a piece of gear.

## Changes
- **Flawless, made clear:** any warned attack, lava, falling stone or scorched ground that hits you breaks it; ordinary bites and shots never do.
- **No fusions or evolutions in the Crucible,** and the cards and rules now say so plainly.
- **The Drum of the Deep's shockwave** is far bigger and hits harder as the waves grow.
- **The pedestal** shows the Ember weapons on offer floating around it, under "Pick a Weapon".
- **The Cape of Embers' health gauge** is now its lava: bright orange at full, cooling toward black as you are hurt.
- **The Ember skin** now dresses your cards and weapons only; gear keeps its own look.
- Jad's Harp is back on the beach.

## Fixes
- Notes from the lava spitters no longer fly on after a wave ends.
- A champion's delayed blow could error after it fell.
