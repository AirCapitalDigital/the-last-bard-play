# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.14.1

## New
- **Favoured Tune,** a new Fountain upgrade: cards you have starred come up more often.
- **Mini bosses wear their names** over their heads, as the Crucible's champions do.
- **Talking in a run is safe:** when a companion or Maestra speaks out in the world, the run pauses until you have read it.

## Changes
- **The Cape of Embers also wards off freezing and poison,** on top of burning roots away: both hold half as long and bite half as hard.
- **An Ent's roots slow you to a crawl** instead of holding you still.
- **The Wolf Husks howl far less,** and more softly.
- Companions now tell you when their great foe hunts you, and the Journal says who to talk to for a foe you have not met.
