# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.14.2

## New
- **Tharrun, reworked.** An opening scene as he tears out of the mountain, and a puzzle for each part: crack the Anvil Fist's bell on the anvil stones, answer the Pyre Hand's song by touching its stones in order, and feed the Maw its own fire. Each part wears its name, his health is back up, and only a few other foes join the fight.
- **Nine new songs:** fast, bright nightcore tracks, each its own: seven join the soundtrack (three free once the harp is found, four as sheet music) and two play on Ember Island. Every one loops seamlessly.
- **Songs shuffle in runs:** the music changes every few minutes (Settings > Audio: on or off, and how often).
- **Skip a song** with the new button beside the music bars, or in the Sanctuary beside the mute button.
- **Curate your soundtrack:** leave any song out of the shuffle from the harp's list.
- **Analog stick speed:** a light tilt of a controller stick walks slower (Settings > Controls).

## Changes
- Auto-aimed weapons only aim at foes on your screen.
- The first run's tour shows the music: foes arrive on the beat, so every song plays a little differently.
- The music-bars switch moved to Settings > Audio.
