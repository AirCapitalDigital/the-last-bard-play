# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.14.4

## Changes
- **The Crescendo of Embers is mythic now, and fiercer:** for a few seconds after you cast it, a ring of flame circles you and burns up any shot that reaches it, and foes it sets alight hit softer and shoot less while they burn.
- The Cape of Embers' description now says that it halves poison and freezing.
