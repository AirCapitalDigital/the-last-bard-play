# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.15.0 "The Sleepers Wake"
*Five ancient keepers sleep under the land. A horn can wake them.*

## New
- **The Challenger's War Horn and five biome challengers.** Andy has an old horn on his wall, and a legend to go with it. Late in a run, blown beside one of the land's sleeping stones, it wakes what slumbers there: the clock stops, the horde falls away, a ring of stones closes in, and you face a challenger alone. One challenge a run.
  - **Sir Halvard, the Frozen Knight** (the tundra): a duel on the beat. Charge into his blade to break his guard, on ice that cracks under you.
  - **The Harvest King** (the meadow): an ancient scarecrow king, shielded by straw effigies that only fire can burn.
  - **Sekhetra, the Sand Colossus** (the desert): a colossus and its heart, burrowing under the sand.
  - **Old Rootmother** (the forest): her roots sing; answer them in order. Every note also has its own colour and shape.
  - **The Lantern Bearer** (the crypts): the circle goes dark. Carry the light to its braziers.
  - They are built to test a fully grown bard, and each pays a title, a head piece to wear, a Journal page and a rare reward of its own.
  - Blown anywhere else, the horn sends you charging forward, safe for its length.
- **Their resting places** stand in every world from your first run: one grand landmark per biome, waiting.
- **Crucible Records:** a slab beside the Tally Stone on Ember Island, and a Crucible tab on the leaderboard, ranks the fastest full Crucible clears.
- **Nine new songs**, each its own piece, and a **song shuffle** for runs (Settings > Audio: on or off, and how often), a **skip-song button** beside the music bars, and a toggle on every song in the harp's list to **leave it out of the shuffle**.
- **Tharrun, reworked:** an opening scene, and a puzzle for each part of the titan.

## Changes
- **The Crescendo of Embers is mythic now:** after a cast a ring of flame burns up incoming shots, and foes it sets alight hit softer.
- **Phone controls:** the joystick no longer gets stuck near the screen's edge, a new setting keeps it where you touch instead, and the Crescendo button can sit on the left (Settings > Controls).
- **Analog stick speed:** a light tilt of a controller stick walks slower (Settings > Controls).
- **The Stalacpipe Organ** is redrawn: crystal stalactites shatter and ring like organ pipes.
- **Blade Paint** carries into any fusion or evolution made from the painted weapon.
- Auto-aimed weapons only aim at foes on your screen.
- The Ember skin dresses your cards only, outside the Crucible.
- The Cape of Embers' description says it halves poison and freezing.

## Fixes
- Crucible climbs no longer land on the run leaderboards.
- Choosing a starting weapon completes its task straight away; tasks say "back at the Sanctuary".
