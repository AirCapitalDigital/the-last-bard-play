# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.16.0 "Every Weapon Its Own Song"
*Every weapon reworked to read at a glance, a place to try them, and a meter to see who's carrying you.*

## New
- **Every weapon, reworked.** All 40 weapons and every evolution and fusion were redrawn so each has its own shape and motion, readable even in a crowded late run:
  - The clutter is gone: no more halo round the bard on fusions, one glint instead of a ring of sparkles, far fewer flames on burning crowds. Every weapon has its own attack colour.
  - Look-alikes now play differently: Iaido leaves X-shaped cuts, the Gong rolls out a bronze wall, Khoomei sends overtone waves in a cone, the Hurdy-Gurdy rolls real wheels, the Longbow fires one great shaft and the Hand Crossbow a fan.
  - Evolutions keep their parents' motions, grander: the Slayer's Horn packs each look like their trick, Pyre Arrows hurls a flaming spear, Ghost Saw sweeps a spectral saw, Reaper's Sling swings scythe heads, the Carillon's bells are cast bronze.
  - **The hounds are remade:** lean, scarred wolves with bared fangs and a proper gallop.
  - Damage is unchanged.
- **The Practice Yard.** A new gate in the Sanctuary's north-west wall, past the companions' homes, leads to a training ground you can see over the wall (it grows with your camp). Pick any weapon, fusion or evolution you have found, at any level, and try it alone on training dummies. Nothing there counts toward anything.
- **Damage meter** (Settings > Gameplay): a few slim bars above your weapon tiles show which weapons did the most damage over the last ten seconds. The pause menu's Stats tab shows the same, and the whole run.

## Changes
- **The biome challengers hit harder and last longer:** built to test a fully grown bard, each is now a real fight of a few minutes. Their openings matter more, and their biggest blows hurt.
