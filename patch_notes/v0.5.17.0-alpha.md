# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.17.0 "The Practice Yard Opens Up"
*A proper testing ground, honest treasure, and a camp that tells you what's new.*

## New
- **The Practice Yard is a real testing ground:**
  - Its walls are the Sanctuary's own, ruined or grand with your camp.
  - A gate to the south-east sets out on a run, just like the Sanctuary's main gate.
  - New options: **Bring my gear and Crescendo**, **Foes use attacks** (dummies throw shots, fans, rings, slams and lunges from across the bestiary, for dodging practice) and **Take damage** (a fall just puts you back on your feet).
  - Dummies fall in a burst of straw and new ones pop in, like a real run; pick their health from light to unbreakable.
- **Treasure tells the truth:** the card stacks rising from a chest are the backs of the real cards you'll be offered, in their colours. A rainbow back means an evolution, blue a fusion, and a fourth card means Lucky Draw came through.
- **The Resonant Stone and the Magic Harp arrive in style:** the first time you come home after finding one, it manifests in the Sanctuary in a column of light, so you know it's there to use.
- **Tasks in the Sanctuary:** the TASKS list now shows in camp too, and Andy's hunt lives at the top of it.

## Changes
- **Now Playing** is a small line under the timer, everywhere, like on Ember Island.
- **The Mirror** shows how to win every title right under its name, and once Andy is in camp, his trophy pieces show as locked tiles with your progress.
- **Phone controls:** the joystick now stays where you first touch by default (Settings > Controls to change it).
- Mini bosses' name tags read DRUM LORD, CHOIR and PHANTOM ORCHESTRA (several can be out at once).
- A Crescendo found in a chest no longer says "(Green)" after its name: the name is already in its colour.

## Fixes
- The damage meter and the off-screen arrows no longer draw over the card scene.
- The challengers' rare rewards show properly on the bard in menus.
