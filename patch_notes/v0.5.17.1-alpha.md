# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.17.1

## New
- **Spectators in the Practice Yard:** each visit, two to four of the friends you've met stand by the wall and watch: they crack jokes, react to what you do, give tips about the weapon in your hand and banter with each other. (Settings > Gameplay to turn them off.)
- **Far more seasonal decoration:** Halloween gets scarecrows, pumpkin patches, cobwebs, bat garlands and haunted lanterns; the Winter Festival now decorates too, with a festival tree, snowmen, gift piles, wreaths and string lights. Both in the Sanctuary (more as it grows) and out on runs.
