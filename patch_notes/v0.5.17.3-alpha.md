# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.17.3

## New
- **A snowy Winter Festival:** during the holidays the Sanctuary and the road are blanketed in snow, roofs and trees wear snowy caps, and gentle snow falls. A grand festival tree glows by the Fountain, coloured lights string along the streets, and far more festive pieces appear: many kinds of presents, wreaths, candy-striped poles, star lanterns, a sleigh, ice sculptures, snow forts, a carolers' stand and more.
- **Smash the season:** presents (Winter Festival) and pumpkins (Halloween) out on the road can be broken like crates and pots. Very rarely, one holds something special.

## Changes
- The holiday welcome shows every hat it gives you.
- Snowmen wear proper top hats.
- Presents no longer sit on top of other things.
