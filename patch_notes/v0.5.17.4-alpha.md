# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.17.4

## New
- **Challenges ask first:** blowing the War Horn beside a sleeping stone now pauses and asks if you're sure. Say yes and the challenger wakes in a short scene (ice cracking, straw and crows lifting, sand pouring, roots tearing, a seal breaking) before the fight begins.
- **The Practice Yard is a tournament ground:** a square arena that grows into painted tournament lists, wooden walls with the Sanctuary's stone on one side (just as you see it over the wall), a spectators' box where your friends gather to watch, and only two gates: home to the Sanctuary, or out on a run.
- **The Tally Stone** on Ember Island now holds the Crucible's fastest clears too: your records on top, names carved below.

## Changes
- The Challenger's War Horn looks like a war horn, and it hangs on Andy's Hunt Board until it's yours.

## Fixes
- The Tally Stone shows wave 63 as your best once you've cleared the Crucible.
