# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.5
*Runs that can start in any land, a Banish that takes only one card, a Sun Burst that clears the air, and a clearer last page of a run.*

## New
- **Start anywhere.** Your first runs still begin in the sunlit Meadow. Once you have finished five runs and found the Resonant Stone, each run can begin in any of the five lands: the Meadow, the Forest, the Snow, the Crypt or the Desert, with their own foes from the first minute. Everything else you go looking for is still placed out in other lands.
- **Pip gets around.** The wild hedgehog can now be found curled up by any village or camp, in every land.

## Changes
- **Banish takes one card.** Banishing a card now swaps only that card for a new one. The other cards stay where they are.
- **Sun Burst clears the air.** As well as blasting the foes around you, it now puffs out every enemy shot.
- **An icy health bar.** While you are chilled, your health bar turns pale frost and says CHILLED, the way it turns green when you are poisoned.
- **MAX means max.** A fully levelled weapon or boost on the HUD always says MAX now, instead of sometimes showing a full row of pips.
- **A tidier death screen.** Your stats and the Echoes you banked share one row, damage sits on the left with your finds and achievements on the right, and the button no longer stretches across the page. A long run's page fits without scrolling. Big numbers get commas.
- **Easier to read on phones.** The callout over your bard (power-ups, Second Wind and friends) is bigger on a phone, with a softer outline on a dark backing, so it stands out from a crowded screen.

## Fixes
- **Fusions on the leaderboard.** Some fusions with long names were left off a run's weapons on the leaderboard. Runs from now on show every weapon they carried.

## Hotfix
- **A new pet: Spek the Rat.** A stubborn rat in a dented pot helmet, and the toughest pet there is: 5% less damage taken and 3% more health. Now and then he pulls out an all-red warhammer and BONKS the nearest foe flat in one hit, knocking back and stunning everything around it. Back at camp he likes to bonk the training dummies (a big crit, every time) and anyone standing nearby. He joins you once you have fallen 15 times.
- **A neater death screen on phones:** tapping a find or a weapon now opens its card fully on screen (it could run off the edge), and achievements fill the space under your damage instead of pushing the page into a scroll.
- **Evolutions hit harder and look bigger.** An evolution now starts at level 5 with extra damage that tapers off as it levels, so evolving never leaves you weaker than the two maxed weapons you gave up, and at max level every evolution does about twice what its parts did. Every evolution strikes with more weight (wind-ups, impact bursts, ground cracks) and throws bigger shots.
- **The Sawwheel Gurdy is reworked:** a glaive's long haft with a spinning saw wheel at its tip sweeps all the way round you and back, slowing everything it hits, then the wheel flies off as rolling saws. Brass Bolts, Swordsong Serenade, Whistling Arrows, Cristal Baschet, Hammered Dulcimer and the Drone Machine also got bigger attacks.
- **The leaderboard on the title screen fills a phone's screen** instead of sitting small in the middle.
