# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.6.0
*A bard who turns as smoothly as you steer, a fuller late game, and evolutions that look the part.*

Versions now have a fourth number: this is 0.5.6.0, and its fixes will be 0.5.6.1, 0.5.6.2 and so on.

## New
- **Sixteen directions.** Your bard now faces sixteen ways instead of eight and turns smoothly from one to the next, and from behind you can see which way your bard is looking.
- **True stick walking.** On a controller or a Steam Deck your bard walks at the exact angle you push the stick, not the nearest of eight.
- **Smoother keyboard walking.** Pressing a new key bends your path into the new direction over a moment instead of snapping. Turning right round is still instant, so a quick dodge back works as before.
- **An arrow for every chest.** Every chest off the screen now has an arrow at the screen's edge with its distance, in its own colour: bronze, silver or gold, crimson for a boss's chest, violet for a keepsake, white-gold for the Closer's Hoard.
- **Seventeen evolutions with bigger attacks:**
  - **Thunder Sheet:** a thunderclap, then lightning strikes the crowd twice.
  - **Zhuge Repeater:** lightning bolts jump from foe to foe, and lightning comes down where they struck.
  - **1812 Overture:** a cannon salvo, then cannonballs crash down on the crowd and set it ablaze.
  - **Bard's Spearsong:** a rain of spears.
  - **Ghost Saw:** three spectral saws hunt the crowd.
  - **Sawbow:** spinning saw wheels that bounce off the edges of the screen.
  - **Panpipe Volley:** two fans of poison darts that burst into clouds, then a rain of darts.
  - **Aeolian Harp:** a great gust, wind slamming down on the crowd and wide columns of light.
  - **Singing Bowl, Mehter March and Pipe Band:** bells, cymbals and drums come down on the crowd.
  - **Capoeira Roda:** the dancers' kicks land out in the crowd.
  - **Charmer's Lute:** four serpents slither out and strike.
  - **Drone Machine, Alphorn Echo, Komuso's Shakuhachi and Swordsong Serenade:** bold rings and wider strikes.

## Changes
- **A fuller late game.** From about ten minutes in, many more foes come and fewer of them shoot. Wailers fire half as often but in fans, together with their neighbours, so there is a lull between volleys. The big bursts now come from the shapes: when a ring or a drum-drawn shape of foes has landed, they stop, flash and fire together. When the screen is already full of shots, the ordinary shooters hold back a little, and the Wailer choir is a rarer set piece.
- **Never weaker for fusing.** Every fusion and every evolution is now at least as strong as the two maxed weapons that made it, right when you make it and at its top level. Several got a bigger head start.
- **Fusions look fused.** A fusion's sparkles now braid in two strands, its own colour and blue, on everything it throws, and when both halves fire together the ring they make is two rings woven together.
