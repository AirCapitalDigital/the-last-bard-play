# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.6.1
*Each weapon now feeds one evolution a run.*

## Changes
- **One evolution per weapon.** Once a weapon has gone into a fusion or an evolution, it will not be offered as a new weapon again for the rest of that run. The Bard's Lute, for example, can evolve with eight different weapons, but now only once a run, so choosing which evolution to make really counts.
- **Your starting weapon stays in hand.** When the weapon you started with is fused, your bard keeps holding it instead of walking on empty-handed.
