# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.6.2
*Golden Echo Chests, three new Fountain upgrades, and ground attacks that no longer hide your foes.*

## New
- **Echo Chests.** Very rarely, any foe may drop a golden Echo Chest. Open it and Echoes pour out: every chest starts as a trickle and may climb up to four more tiers, each one bigger and louder than the last, all the way to a JACKPOT. Each chest pays between 20 and 200 Echoes.
- **Echo Hunter** (Fountain upgrade, 5 levels): Echo Chests turn up a good deal more often with each level, and far more often in Loot Mode.
- **Skip** (Fountain upgrade, 4 levels): a new button beside Banish and Reroll that passes on a whole card offer and takes nothing. One skip a run for each level. Keyboard K, controller RB.
- **Booster Pack** (Fountain upgrade, bought once): every run opens with a pick of any boost card, shown as a grid of icons, before the first foes reach you.

## Changes
- **Ground attacks stay on the ground.** Pools, rings, blasts and other area attacks (the Rainstick, the Theorbo, the War Drum and many evolutions) are now drawn under your foes and bosses instead of over them, so a big late-game build never hides what you are fighting.
- **More arrows on chests for that one playtester, you know who you are.** Every chest now has its own bobbing arrow: bronze, silver, gold, the boss chest and the new Echo Chest each have their own.
- The Fountain's upgrades page now scrolls to fit the new upgrades.
