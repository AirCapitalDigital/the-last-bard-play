# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.6.4
*Kinder choices in the camp.*

## Changes
- **Look before you take.** When a gift lets you choose one item, tapping it now shows it first, with **Back** next to **Keep It**, so you can look at every choice before you commit. On phones, a single tap used to take it straight away.
- **Hand them all over at once.** When you have several things to trade in, a new **Trade All** button turns them all in together and shows what you got one after another, instead of a conversation for each one.
