# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.6.5
*A balance hotfix.*

## Changes
- **Thieves pay it all back.** Catch a Lute Goblin and every chest it stole drops back out, the very same chest it took. Only the XP and Echoes it carried are still halved.
- **More Crescendo Scores.** Scores drop about 40% more often from every kind of chest, and a drop no longer goes to waste on a rarity you have already completed.
- **Booster Pack is cheaper.** The Fountain's Booster Pack now costs 7,500 Echoes (was 15,000).
- **A gentler Rally Horn.** At high attack speed the horn sounds less often and more softly. The attack itself is unchanged.
- **A shorter late fight.** One of the longest fights at the end of a run has a little less health.
