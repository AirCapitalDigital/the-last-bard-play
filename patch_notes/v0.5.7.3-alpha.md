# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.7.3
*Honk.*

## New
- **Doug the Goose.** A new pet: a small goose who will not put his wooden bat down. He makes you a little faster and a little more likely to land critical hits, says "Honk!" now and then, and every so often honks, charges and swings for the fences at whatever is closest. In the camp he bonks the training dummies, and the residents. Smash 600 crates and pots to win him over.

## Changes
- **A grander Leaderboard.** The camp's Leaderboard is now a proper hall of fame: a gilded board on a stone dais with lanterns, a laurel crest and a winners' podium.
