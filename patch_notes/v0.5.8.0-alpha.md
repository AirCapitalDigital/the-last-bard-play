# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.8.0 "The Hunt"
*A slayer-master comes to camp, and every foe is worth counting.*

## New
- **Andy the Beastbreaker.** A young slayer-master moves into the Sanctuary a few runs after Kage joins you, and sets up a Hunt Board in front of the armory's forge.
- **Hunts.** Andy offers three hunts to choose from: slay a number of one foe, slay them with one music family, or spend time in one land. A small slate on screen shows how far along you are. Walk back to Andy to hand a hunt in for **Marks**. Every 10th hunt in a row pays triple, every 50th ten times over. Don't like the offer? A swap costs 10 Marks and your streak.
- **Ten hunter ranks.** Marks earned raise your rank, from Whelp all the way to Mythical Beast Slayer. Each rank is a title you can wear and pays a lump of Marks, and the Hunt Board grows grander as you climb.
- **War Paint.** Buy one paint with Marks before a run and it shows on your bard's face. Blade Paint: a weapon you choose turns up more often and arrives at level 2, hitting 10% harder. Beast Paint: a boost you choose turns up more often and starts a level higher. Eye Paint: your first level-up offers only new weapons from the music family you chose. If the run ends before 2:00, the paint is refunded.
- **Hunter's cards.** Nine new cards that only turn up in runs once you have bought them from Andy:
  - **Falconer's Whistle.** A hawk circles, stoops through a line of foes and carries far orbs and Echoes home.
  - **Bullroarer.** A whirled slat on a widening circle drags foes onto it and cuts them.
  - **Jaw Harp.** Snares that root foes and draw them together.
  - **Bone Rattle.** Kills leave bone shards that burst and chain through a crowd.
  - **Slayer's Horn.** A howl sends ordinary foes running, then a pack of spectral hounds pins, bites and drags down the strongest.
  - Four new boosts: **Trophy Hunter**, **Butcher's Due**, **Killing Song** and **Predator's Stride**.
- **Trophies.** Every foe, the three mini bosses included, has three kill milestones, 72 in all. Reach one and Andy hands you a piece to wear: a cape, a tunic, boots, something for your back, and for the last milestone a head piece themed on that foe. Each comes with a Marks bonus. If you have already slain thousands, he hands over everything you have earned at once.
- **More to wear at the Mirror.** Vanity now has five slots: head, cape, chest, legs and back. Pieces are chosen under slot tabs and are worn over your gear without changing its stats.
