# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.9.0 "Clear Picks"
*Cards you can read at a glance, and a pause that feels like one.*

## New
- **New card designs.** Weapons and boosts now look different before you read a word:
  - **Weapons:** gold on deep red, with a pointed arch around the instrument.
  - **Boosts:** silver on midnight blue, with the picture in a laurel wreath in its music family's colour. The wreath sways gently.
  - **Fusions:** steel blue, with the two weapons side by side under twin arches.
  - **Evolutions:** gold and royal purple, with a rose window, a rainbow edge and both music families named.
  - **Companions:** bronze in the companion's own colour, with their portrait in an oval frame.

  Every card keeps its ornate songbook details.
- **Read a card faster.**
  - What the card does now comes first, in big type, with the details smaller underneath.
  - Each card names its music family beside its symbol.
  - The "Boosts your" / "Boosted by your" row at the bottom glows green and has room for more icons.
- **Know what works with what.** Point at one of those little icons (or tap it on a phone) and it names its card. With a controller, the names show while the card is highlighted.
- **How It Works.** On your very first run, a short guide explains the basics at your first level-up:
  - just move and dodge;
  - weapons and boosts work on their own;
  - there is nothing to equip.

## Changes
- **The level-up screen clearly shows the game is paused.** The world behind the cards turns grey and still, the music softens as if behind glass, and a line under the title says "Game paused · take your time".
- **A new weapon shows itself.** When you pick a new weapon, its picture and name rise over your bard as it starts playing.
