# The Last Bard: Alpha 0.5.9.1
*Sharper attacks, and safer card picks.*

## Changes
- **Twelve weapons hit the ground in style.** Each one now leaves its own mark:
  - **Hurdy-Gurdy:** toothed crank wheels grind across the ground.
  - **Rainstick:** a downpour fills a spreading puddle.
  - **Gong:** a bronze sunburst rolls out.
  - **Sitar:** gourd-shaped pools ring with strings.
  - **War Drum:** a drumhead shockwave, ropes and pegs and all.
  - **Soprano's Shriek:** shatters like glass.
  - **Harp of Light:** burns harp glyphs into the ground.
  - **Atlatl:** spears land in cracked craters.
  - **Ballad:** a ribbon of music and rose petals circles you.
  - **Berimbau and Kora:** stamp their instruments where they land.
  - **Yodel:** echoes out like mountain ridges.

  Only the looks changed: damage and reach are the same.
- **Morwen casts three spells.** An Arcane Sigil, a falling star, and a swirling Resonance Orb, in turn.

## New
- **Choose which keys pick a card.** Settings > Controls has three key rows:
  - **Crescendo key:** fires the Crescendo.
  - **Action key:** confirms in menus and picks a card. It can now be a different key from the Crescendo key.
  - **Pick a card with:** "Enter only" or "Action key or Enter" stop a key you are spamming for the Crescendo from picking a level-up card by accident.

  Number keys, clicks, taps and controllers always work.

## Hotfix
- **Clearer cards.** The green up arrow is back beside every gain on the cards, and card text is now the same size on every card instead of shrinking on crowded ones.
- **Know where you are.** The name of the land you are in now shows beside your health bar, in the same words Andy's hunts use.
- **Tidier cards.** The highlight glow lines up with the card's corners, and the "Boosts your" label sits on its own tab so the icons have more room.
- **Phones:** tapping a card in Andy's shop or a War Paint no longer buys it; only the Buy and Paint It buttons do. The War Paint menu keeps one size, and tapping vanity pieces at the Mirror no longer jumps the page back to the top.
- **Steadier treasure cards on phones.** Cards no longer shrink their picture or jump in size as a chest's cards appear, and "Fuses with" / "Evolves with" now sit in the green box with your boosts, at full size.
- **Hunts are easier to follow.** A finished hunt now says "Hunt Complete", and your current hunt also shows in the camp and in the pause menu. Big foes like the Abomination now fit in the hunt's little picture.
- **Ents spread out their slams** instead of stacking them on one spot.
- **More phone fixes:** tapping a boost in the Booster Pack no longer takes it (press Take It), and the Mirror no longer jumps back to the top when you tap a vanity piece.
